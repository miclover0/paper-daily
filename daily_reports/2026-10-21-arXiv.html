A1 ChartEvo: Self-Evolving Agent Harnesses for Long-Tail Chart Reasoning

arXiv: 2610.28282 QwenRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have substantially advanced chart understanding and reasoning, yet their performance remains highly uneven across chart types. 多模态大型语言模型（ MLLM ）具有相当先进的图表理解和推理能力，但它们在各种图表类型中的表现仍然非常不平衡。

While modern MLLMs perform well on common charts, their accuracy drops sharply on long-tail charts with diverse visual structures and reasoning requirements. 虽然现代传销在常见图表上表现良好，但在具有不同视觉结构和推理要求的长尾图表上，其准确性急剧下降。

Tool-augmented agents can improve complex chart reasoning by introducing specialized visual capabilities, but manually engineering strategies and tools for the heterogeneous and evolving long tail is difficult to scale. 工具增强代理可以通过引入专门的视觉功能来改进复杂的图表推理，但针对异构且不断发展的长尾手动设计策略和工具很难扩展。

We introduce \textbf{ChartEvo}, a recursive self-improvement framework that enables chart agents to adapt to underperforming chart types through experience-driven harness-level self-improvement. 我们引入了\ textbf {ChartEvo} ，这是一个递归的自我提升框架，使图表代理能够通过体验驱动的线束级自我提升来适应表现不佳的图表类型。

ChartEvo jointly evolves reusable chart-specific Skills and executable Tools, while learning reusable local tool-call chains to compose evolved capabilities and control the inference overhead of tool-augmented reasoning. ChartEvo联合开发可重用的图表特定技能和可执行工具，同时学习可重用的本地工具调用链，以编写演进的功能并控制工具增强推理的推理开销。

On long-tail charts from ChartBench and ChartX, ChartEvo improves accuracy over its base MLLM, Qwen3.7-Plus, by \textbf{45.6\%} and \textbf{32.5\%}, respectively, demonstrating the effectiveness of recursive self-improvement for long-tail chart adaptation. 在ChartBench和ChartX的长尾图上， ChartEvo分别比其基础MLLM Qwen3.7-Plus提高了\ textbf {45.6\ %}和\ textbf {32.5\ %}的准确度，证明了递归自我改进对长尾图适应的有效性。

A2 S-Agent 2.0: Spatial Reasoning with a General Coding Agent

arXiv: 2610.28354 AnthropicGoogleQwen

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Spatial reasoning asks a model to work out where objects are in 3D space, how they relate to one another, and how they move. 空间推理要求模型确定物体在3D空间中的位置、它们如何相互关联以及它们如何移动。

Agentic VLMs have recently brought large improvements on such tasks by letting the model actively gather evidence with 3D perception tools instead of answering in a single pass. Agentic VLM最近对此类任务进行了大幅改进，让模型使用3D感知工具主动收集证据，而不是一次性回答。

Even so, on many questions the tools return the right evidence and the model still does not arrive at the correct answer: the gap between model and tools, where raw readings become usable conclusions, is left unfilled. 即便如此，在许多问题上，工具返回了正确的证据，而模型仍然没有得到正确的答案：模型和工具之间的差距，原始读数成为可用的结论，没有填补。

We present S-Agent 2.0, a spatial reasoning agent that equips an off-the-shelf general coding agent with a command-line perception toolkit, and we design the interface around one principle: help the VLM close the evidence loop: acquire scene evidence, remember it in a persistent scene memory, and interpret each reading in plain spatial language. 我们提出了S-Agent 2.0 ，这是一种空间推理代理，它为现成的通用编码代理配备了命令行感知工具包，我们围绕一个原则设计了界面：帮助VLM关闭证据循环：获取场景证据，将其记忆在持久的场景记忆中，并以简单的空间语言解释每次阅读。

This design keeps the full flexibility of a coding agent, and every reading it acts on stays grounded in the tools' geometry. 这种设计保持了编码代理的充分灵活性，并且每次读取都以工具的几何形状为基础。

On MMSI-Bench and ReVSI, the toolkit improves a strong open-source model, Qwen3.8-27B, by +13.9 and +20.5 points over the same agent without tools, and a strong closed-source model, Gemini-3.8-Flash, by +11.2 and +9.6 points. 在MMSI-Bench和ReVSI上，该工具包将强大的开源模型Qwen3.8-27B在没有工具的情况下比同一代理提高+13.9和+20.5分，并将强大的闭源模型Gemini-3.8-Flash提高+11.2和+9.6分。

With the open backbone S-Agent 2.0 reaches 66.5 on MMSI-Bench and 82.3 on ReVSI, ahead of the specialized system SpatialClaw on the same backbone (62.6 and 74.5), and with Gemini-3.8-Flash it reaches 74.1 and 90.5 against its 67.0 and 79.7. 借助开放主干网， S-Agent 2.0在MMSI-Bench上达到66.5 ，在ReVSI上达到82.3 ，领先于同一主干网（ 62.6和74.5 ）上的专用系统SpatialClaw ，借助Gemini-3.8-Flash ，其达到74.1和90.5 ，而其67.0和79.7。

Both tool gains shrink as the backbone grows more capable, but SpatialClaw's shrinks far more on ReVSI, from +13.2 to +2.7, while ours stays at +9.6. 随着骨干能力的增强，这两种工具都会收缩，但SpatialClaw在ReVSI上的收缩幅度要大得多，从+13.2收缩到+2.7 ，而我们的收缩幅度则保持在+9.6。

S-Agent 2.0 also works across different general coding agent harnesses, such as Codex and Claude Code. S-Agent 2.0还适用于不同的通用编码代理线束，例如Codex和Claude Code。

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Small defects in unfamiliar product categories can be difficult to distinguish from benign appearance variations in a global view. 在全球范围内，很难将不熟悉的产品类别中的小缺陷与良性外观变化区分开来。

Additional reasoning over the same view may leave this ambiguity unresolved. 对同一视图的其他推理可能会使这种模棱两可的问题得不到解决。

We introduce IndusAgent, a Qwen3-VL-8B inspection policy trained through supervised fine-tuning and tool-augmented reinforcement learning for cross-category industrial anomaly detection. 我们引入了IndusAgent ，这是一种Qwen3-VL-8B检测策略，通过监督微调和工具增强强化学习进行培训，用于跨类别工业异常检测。

Indus-CoT provides 3,000 trajectories from 26 Real-IAD categories, generated with actual tool feedback and screened against annotations after teacher generation. Indus-CoT提供来自26个Real-IAD类别的3,000条轨迹，这些轨迹通过实际工具反馈生成，并在教师生成后对照注释进行筛选。

GRPO optimizes an accuracy-gated objective that conditions localization, type, and tool rewards, including call costs, on final diagnostic correctness while retaining an independent format reward. GRPO优化了一个精度门控目标，该目标在最终诊断正确性方面为本地化、类型和工具奖励（包括呼叫成本）提供条件，同时保留独立的格式奖励。

Across three training seeds, the five-benchmark mean balanced accuracy is 80.5\% for a weight-matched ungated control and 83.5% for the gated policy. 在三个训练种子中，对于重量匹配的无门控控制，五基准平均平衡准确率为80.5\ % ，对于门控策略，平均平衡准确率为83.5%。

Evaluation uses disjoint product categories and a fixed defect vocabulary. 评估使用不相交的产品类别和固定的缺陷词汇。

A4 CoEvo-RSI: When Harnesses Evolve, Which Models Should Follow?

arXiv: 2610.25078 DeepSeekQwenRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Model--harness co-evolution has emerged as a prominent paradigm for recursive self-improving (RSI) agents, jointly evolving their execution scaffolds and intrinsic capabilities. 模型- Harness协同进化已成为递归自我改善（ RSI ）代理的突出范例，共同发展其执行支架和内在能力。

We study the persistent yet often overlooked brain--body mismatch problem in this paradigm, where an evolving harness (body) demands behaviors its model (brain) has not learned or restricts capabilities it already possesses. 我们研究了这种范式中持续存在但经常被忽视的大脑-身体错配问题，在这种范式中，不断发展的线束（身体）要求其模型（大脑）尚未学习的行为或限制其已经拥有的能力。

We introduce CoEvo-RSI, a RSI framework that advances model--harness co-evolution through agent-driven, bidirectional historical inheritance. 我们引入了CoEvo-RSI ，这是一个RSI框架，它通过代理驱动的双向历史继承来推进模型- Harness协同进化。

Drawing on a shared evolutionary archive and execution feedback, a meta-agent coordinates harness development from historical designs with model checkpoint selection or merging and subsequent adaptation under the selected harness, aligning inherited capabilities with evolving execution requirements to reduce brain--body mismatch. 借助共享的进化存档和执行反馈，元代理通过模型检查点选择或合并以及所选线束下的后续适应来协调历史设计中的线束开发，使继承的能力与不断变化的执行要求保持一致，以减少脑-身体不匹配。

Using only supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on its own rejection-sampled trajectories, CoEvo-RSI makes Qwen3.5-9B competitive with larger models across diverse vertical applications, including multi-agent failure attribution, role-aware text-to-SQL, and smart-home instruction following. CoEvo-RSI仅在其自身的剔除采样轨迹上使用监督微调（ SFT ） ，使Qwen3.5-9B在不同的垂直应用中与更大的模型竞争，包括多代理失败归因、角色感知文本到SQL和智能家居指令跟踪。

It surpasses DeepSeek-V4-Pro on TraceElephant (+14.16 agent accuracy) and GLM-5.3 on HomeBench VM (+4.90 success rate), underscoring the need for brain--body alignment and the potential of bidirectional historical inheritance to advance agent co-evolution. 它超过了TraceElephant上的DeepSeek-V4-Pro （ +14.16药剂准确度）和HomeBench VM上的GLM-5.3 （ +4.90成功率） ，强调了大脑-身体对齐的必要性以及双向历史遗传的潜力，以促进药剂的共同进化。

A5 Zephyrus-R1: Agentic Reinforcement Learning for Weather Reporting

arXiv: 2610.25625 OpenAIQwenKimi

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as a key method for training AI models in scientific applications, but RL remains difficult when outputs cannot easily be verified against a known ground-truth answer. 强化学习（ RL ）已成为在科学应用中训练人工智能模型的关键方法，但当无法根据已知的地面真实答案轻松验证输出时， RL仍然很困难。

Natural-language weather forecast reports are one such domain: they translate atmospheric data into assessments of weather evolution, hazards, and uncertainty for both the general public and key decision-makers. 自然语言天气预报报告就是这样一个领域：它们将大气数据转化为公众和关键决策者对天气演变、灾害和不确定性的评估。

Unlike numerical weather-analysis questions, they admit many valid outputs, which limits deterministic verification. 与数值天气分析问题不同，它们承认许多有效的输出，这限制了确定性验证。

To address this, we develop Zephyrus-R1, a practical framework for RL on scientific weather forecast reporting. 为了解决这个问题，我们开发了Zephyrus-R1 ，这是科学天气预报报告的实用框架。

We focus on three key components of the agentic RL pipeline: reward design, curriculum learning, and an analysis of human-expert alignment. 我们专注于代理RL管道的三个关键组成部分：奖励设计、课程学习和人与专家对齐分析。

To encourage both report legibility and factual accuracy, we design a reward function that combines a meteorological rubric with bidirectional claim entailment, both evaluated against National Weather Service reference reports. 为了鼓励报告清晰度和事实准确性，我们设计了一个奖励函数，将气象分类和双向索赔相结合，两者都根据国家气象局参考报告进行了评估。

Our framework optimizes this reward in a simulated tool-calling environment, enabling vision-language agents to gather and analyze forecast evidence across regions and lead times before producing a report. 我们的框架在模拟工具调用环境中优化此奖励，使视觉语言代理能够在生成报告之前收集和分析跨地区和交付周期的预测证据。

We use annotations from three meteorologists to audit the claim labels produced by our LLM judges. 我们使用三位气象学家的注释来审核法学硕士评委制作的索赔标签。

Finally, a curriculum helps small models first develop tool use and scientific reasoning through RL on verifiable weather-analysis questions, then optimize the open-ended report reward. 最后，课程帮助小模型首先通过RL在可验证的天气分析问题上开发工具使用和科学推理，然后优化开放式报告奖励。

After training Qwen3.5-4B with Zephyrus-R1, we improve the mean across six judge-metric scores by 26% over the base model and achieve results competitive with frontier open-weight models. 在使用Zephyrus-R1训练Qwen3.5-4B之后，我们将六个判断指标的平均分比基本模型提高了26% ，并实现了与前沿开放权重模型竞争的结果。

For Qwen3.8-27B, we improve the same mean by 13% over the base model, by 6.7% over Kimi-K3, and by 2.7% over GPT-5.6-Luna. 对于Qwen3.8-27B ，我们将相同的平均值比基本模型提高了13% ，比Kimi-K3提高了6.7% ，比GPT-5.6-Luna提高了2.7%。

A6 HarnessPro: Recursive Harness Self-Improvement with Pareto Optimization for Performance-Cost Trade-offs

arXiv: 2610.26770 OpenAIAnthropicRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Harness development plays a crucial role in building capable AI agents. 线束开发在构建有能力的人工智能代理中起着至关重要的作用。

Recent approaches improve harnesses through recursive self-improvement (RSI), but their performance gains often come with higher execution costs. 最近的方法通过递归自我改进（ RSI ）来改进线束，但其性能提升通常伴随着更高的执行成本。

We introduce HarnessPro, a label-free method for evolving harnesses to improve task performance while reducing execution cost. 我们引入了HarnessPro ，这是一种无标签的方法，用于改进线束，以提高任务性能，同时降低执行成本。

In this setting, candidate harnesses are evaluated using LLM-generated tests without access to ground-truth solutions or official tests. 在此设置中，候选线束使用LLM生成的测试进行评估，而无需访问地面真相解决方案或官方测试。

Specialized agents diagnose execution trajectories, plan updates with different performance-cost priorities, and implement them as candidate harnesses. 专业代理诊断执行轨迹，使用不同的绩效成本优先级计划更新，并将其作为候选工具来实施。

Rather than returning a single harness, HarnessPro uses normalized weighted squared-distance selection to provide cost-focused (lite), balanced (medium), and performance-focused (heavy) harnesses for different deployment budgets. HarnessPro使用标准化加权平方距离选择，而不是返回单个线束，为不同的部署预算提供以成本为中心（精简版）、平衡型（中型）和以性能为中心（重型）的线束。

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

A general observation in agentic workflows, especially bounded ones, is that the decision space of the LLM, when we separate a decision from generation, starts approaching saturation: the agent meets the same states again and again, and after enough conversations it stops meeting new ones.

LLMs generally don't treat these decisions as an optimization problem and instead re-derive them from their context/prompt on every turn.

For Claude Opus 5.5, which solves 96% of tasks on its own, running it as a BLM cuts model calls and cost per task by about 98% and lets it solve 2 more tasks.

On InventoryBench, the solved policy with no model beats every published method.

Where the hard part is writing content rather than choosing moves, such as resolving SymPy issues, few decisions can be solved in advance, and a BLM helps little.

A1 REVEAL: Robust Evolution of Vision-Language Models for Explainable AI-Video Detection

arXiv: 2502.14994 OpenAIGoogleQwen

📝 中文摘要（语步标注·中英对照）：

【研究空白】The rapid advancement of AI-generated video poses challenges to digital authenticity and security. 人工智能生成的视频的快速发展对数字真实性和安全性构成了挑战。

【研究空白】Current detection methods, often trained on specific datasets, struggle with the ever-evolving landscape of generative techniques and unseen manipulations. 当前的检测方法通常在特定数据集上进行培训，难以应对不断发展的生成技术和看不见的操作。

【提出方法】We introduce a framework leveraging Vision Language Models (VLMs) for robust AI-generated video detection. 我们引入了一个利用视觉语言模型（ VLM ）的框架，用于强大的人工智能生成的视频检测。

【提出方法】Our approach equips the VLM with the ability to reason about video content and use external tools to identify subtle inconsistencies, mirroring human system 2 thinking. 我们的方法使VLM能够对视频内容进行推理，并使用外部工具来识别微妙的不一致性，从而反映人类系统2的思维。

【研究背景】Our self-evolving VLM dynamically selects and composes appropriate tools, enhancing its ability to generalize to novel video generation techniques. 我们的自我进化VLM动态选择和组合适当的工具，增强其推广到新颖视频生成技术的能力。

【研究背景】The modular design promotes interpretability, allowing for a clearer understanding of VLM's decision-making process. 模块化设计提高了可解释性，从而更清楚地了解VLM的决策过程。

【技术路线】To evaluate, we establish the first benchmark VidForensic containing 1.4k+ high-quality AI-generated videos across eight generative models. 为了进行评估，我们在八个生成模型中建立了第一个包含1.4k+高质量AI生成视频的基准VidForensic。

【实验设置】Experiments show that REVEAL improves F1 scores by 9.1% to 30.2% over top baselines across our datasets for VLMs, notably for GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5 pro, and QWen-VL-Max, and Llava-One-Vision-7B. 实验表明，在我们的VLM数据集中， REVEL将F1分数比最高基线提高了9.1%至30.2% ，尤其是GPT-4o、Gemini 1.5 pro和QWen-VL-Max以及Llava-One-Vision-7B。

【研究空白】While open-world AI-video detection remains an open challenge, our results indicate that existing methods fail primarily because they lack tool-enabled, higher-order reasoning. 虽然开放世界的人工智能视频检测仍然是一个公开的挑战，但我们的结果表明，现有方法之所以失败，主要是因为它们缺乏工具支持的高阶推理。

A2 SHARPO: Segment-Level Credit Assignment for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

arXiv: 2610.00838 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（语步标注·中英对照）：

【研究背景】Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) trains a large language model (LLM) to act over long, multi-step interactions. Agentic强化学习（ RL ）训练大型语言模型（ LLM ） ，以应对长时间、多步骤的交互。

【研究空白】However, a single localized error can cause task failure, while trajectory-level rewards provide limited guidance for assigning credit to individual decisions. 然而，单个局部错误可能导致任务失败，而轨迹级奖励为个人决策分配信用提供有限的指导。

【研究目的】To address this limitation, we introduce Segment-level Hindsight Advantage Reweighting for Policy Optimization (SHARPO), a credit-assignment mechanism that refines Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) at the level of environment-facing segments. 为了解决这一限制，我们引入了针对政策优化的细分级事后优势重新加权（ SHARPO ） ，这是一种信用分配机制，在面向环境的细分级层面完善了集团相对政策优化（ GRPO ）。

【研究空白】Inspired by the existing on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) method, SHARPO computes teacher-student log-probability gaps within each segment and uses the resulting signal to compute a bounded multiplier on the GRPO advantage. 受现有政策自馏（ OPSD ）方法的启发， SHARPO计算每个细分市场中的师生对数概率差距，并使用所得信号计算GRPO优势的有界乘数。

【技术路线】This multiplier is shared by all tokens within the segment, allowing credit to vary across different segments. 此乘数由细分市场内的所有代币共享，允许不同细分市场之间的信用额度有所不同。

【实验结果】With Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, SHARPO outperforms existing baselines on the ALFWorld and WebShop benchmarks, including GRPO, SDAR, RLSD, and StepOPSD. 使用Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct ， SHARPO的表现优于ALFWorld和WebShop基准的现有基线，包括GRPO、SDAR、RLSD和StepOPSD。

A3 GUI-HARVEST: Self-Improving GUI Agents through Evidence-Driven Harness Evolution

arXiv: 2610.00948 OpenAIQwenRSI

📝 中文摘要（语步标注·中英对照）：

【研究背景】The executable harness surrounding a GUI model determines how observations are assembled, actions are executed, and verification, recovery, and termination are controlled. 围绕GUI模型的可执行线束决定了如何组装观察、执行操作以及控制验证、恢复和终止。

【研究空白】Compared with harness optimization for non-GUI agents, automatically optimizing this harness poses three coupled challenges: reconciling model intent with observed visual effects, diagnosing failures under variable execution outcomes, and identifying recurrent failure patterns across tasks and translating them into reusable runtime changes. 与非GUI代理的线束优化相比，自动优化此线束带来了三个耦合的挑战：协调模型意图与观察到的视觉效果，诊断可变执行结果下的故障，以及识别跨任务的复发故障模式并将其转换为可重用的运行时更改。

【提出方法】We introduce GUI-HARVEST, an automatic harness optimizer that enables self-improving GUI agents with frozen backbone models. 我们引入了GUI-HARVEST ，这是一种自动线束优化器，可使用冻结的骨干模型自我改进GUI代理。

【技术路线】First, to ground diagnosis in observed action effects, it aligns model outputs and executed actions with before-and-after screenshots, tying findings to specific interface transitions. 首先，为了在观察到的动作效果中进行地面诊断，它将模型输出和执行动作与之前和之后的屏幕截图对齐，将发现与特定的界面转换联系起来。

【技术路线】Second, to account for execution variability, it treats repeated runs of the same task as a joint evidence unit, using within-task comparisons to locate outcome-relevant behavioral differences. 其次，为了考虑执行变异性，它将同一任务的重复运行视为联合证据单元，使用任务内比较来定位与结果相关的行为差异。

【实验结果】Third, it consolidates verified findings across tasks into recurring failure patterns, maps them to bounded source-code edits with predictions recorded before evaluation, and checks the predicted behavioral effects alongside task performance through repeated execution. 第三，它将跨任务的验证结果整合到重复的失败模式中，将其映射到有界的源代码编辑，并在评估之前记录预测，并通过重复执行来检查预测的行为效果和任务性能。

【实验设置】Experiments on OSWorld-Verified show consistent held-out gains across six general-purpose open, GUI-specialized open, and proprietary backbone models; Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct gains 12.33 points on the full suite. OSWorld-Verified的实验表明，六种通用开放式、GUI专用开放式和专有骨干模型获得了一致的增益； Qwen3-VL-32B-Instruct在全套产品上获得了12.33分。

【实验结果】Frozen-harness transfer improves GPT-5 by 13.87 percentage points on WindowsAgentArena at 50 steps without further optimization. Frozen-harness transfer使WindowsAgentArena上的GPT-5在50步时提高了13.87个百分点，而无需进一步优化。

【实验结果】With the same backbone and initial harness, GUI-HARVEST outperforms Self-Harness and Meta-Harness, suggesting that GUI-specific diagnosis and validation help harness improvements generalize to unseen tasks. 使用相同的主干和初始线束， GUI-HARVEST的性能优于Self-Harness和Meta-Harness ，这表明GUI特定的诊断和验证有助于利用改进推广到看不见的任务。

【研究背景】The code is available at https://github.com/GaryYang12345/GUI-HARVEST. 该代码可在https://github.com/GaryYang12345/GUI-HARVEST上获得。

A5 Detecting Multi-Agent Collusion Through Multi-Agent Interpretability

arXiv: 2604.01151 MetaDeepSeekQwen

📝 中文摘要（语步标注·中英对照）：

【应用前景】As LLM agents are increasingly deployed in multi-agent systems, they introduce risks of covert coordination that may evade standard forms of human oversight. 随着LLM代理越来越多地部署在多代理系统中，它们引入了可能逃避标准形式的人类监督的秘密协调风险。

【研究空白】While linear probes on model activations have shown promise for detecting deception in single-agent settings, collusion is inherently a multi-agent phenomenon, and the use of internal representations for detecting collusion between agents remains unexplored. 虽然模型激活的线性探针已显示出在单代理设置中检测欺骗的前景，但勾结本质上是一种多代理现象，并且使用内部表示来检测代理之间的勾结仍未得到探索。

【提出方法】We introduce NARCBench, a benchmark for evaluating collusion detection under environment distribution shift, and propose five probing techniques that aggregate per-agent deception scores to classify scenarios at the group level, evaluated across four open-weight models (Qwen3-32B, Llama-3.1-70B, DeepSeek-R1 32B, GPT-OSS-20B) and six probe architectures. 我们引入了NARCBench ，这是评估环境分布变化下的勾结检测的基准，并提出了五种探测技术，汇总每个代理的欺骗分数，以便在组级别对场景进行分类，这些技术在四个开放权重模型（ Qwen3-32B ， Llama-3.1-70B ， DeepSeek-R1 32B ， GPT-OSS-20B ）和六个探测架构中进行了评估。

【研究空白】We frame this as a distributed anomaly detection problem, identifying three collusion signatures that map onto distinct anomaly types and detection paradigms. 我们将其视为分布式异常检测问题，识别映射到不同异常类型和检测范式的三个勾结特征。

【实验结果】Every model reaches 1.00 AUROC in-distribution; on our strongest model (Llama-3.1-70B), our five probing techniques achieve 0.73 to 0.93 AUROC when transferred zero-shot to structurally different multi-agent scenarios and 0.99 to 1.00 on a steganographic blackjack card-counting task, with detection performance scaling with model capability. 每个模型都达到1.00 AUROC分布；在我们最强的模型（ Llama-3.1-70B ）上，我们的五种探测技术在将零点转移到结构不同的多代理场景时实现0.73至0.93 AUROC ，在隐写二十一点卡片计数任务中实现0.99至1.00 ，并具有模型功能的检测性能缩放。

【主要贡献】We find that no single probing technique dominates across all collusion types, consistent with the framework's prediction that different anomaly types require different detection paradigms. 我们发现，没有单一的探测技术在所有勾结类型中占主导地位，这与框架的预测一致，即不同的异常类型需要不同的检测范式。

【研究目的】This work takes a step toward multi-agent interpretability: extending white-box inspection from single models to multi-agent contexts, where detection requires aggregating signals across agents. 这项工作朝着多Agent可解释性迈出了一步：将白盒检查从单个模型扩展到多Agent上下文，其中检测需要跨Agent聚合信号。

【研究背景】These results suggest that model internals provide a complementary signal to text-level monitoring for detecting multi-agent collusion. 这些结果表明，模型内部为文本级监控提供了补充信号，用于检测多智能体勾结。

【研究背景】Code and data available at https://github.com/aaronrose227/narcbench. 代码和数据可在https://github.com/aaronrose227/narcbench上获得。

A6 Kernel-Managed Shared Memory for System-Wide Personalization

arXiv: 2609.10144 OpenAIMetaQwen

📝 中文摘要（语步标注·中英对照）：

【研究空白】AI systems become more useful when they can adapt to the people using them, but in multi-agent systems, useful context learned by one agent often remains unavailable to others. 当人工智能系统能够适应使用它们的人时，它们会变得更有用，但在多智能体系统中，一个智能体学习的有用上下文通常对其他人不可用。

【提出方法】We present kernel-managed shared memory, a system-level abstraction in which specialized agents write structured, tagged memories while the agent-system kernel, not individual agents, governs retrieval, privacy enforcement, and prompt injection. 我们提出了内核管理的共享内存，这是一种系统级抽象，其中专业代理编写结构化的标记内存，而代理系统内核而不是单个代理管理检索、隐私强制和提示注入。

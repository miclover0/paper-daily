A1 Payoff scaling shapes cooperation in LLM agents across languages

arXiv: 2601.19082 AnthropicGoogleAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents that negotiate, coordinate, and make decisions on behalf of human users. 大型语言模型（ LLM ）越来越多地被部署为代表人类用户进行协商、协调和决策的自主代理。

Understanding how they cooperate is therefore important for AI governance and machine behaviour research. 因此，了解他们如何合作对于人工智能治理和机器行为研究非常重要。

We examine two factors: payoff magnitude and interaction language. 我们研究了两个因素：回报幅度和互动语言。

Classical game theory predicts that multiplying all payoffs by a positive constant should not change behaviour because the underlying strategic incentives remain unchanged. 经典博弈论预测，将所有收益乘以正常数不应改变行为，因为潜在的战略激励保持不变。

We test this prediction using five widely adopted commercial LLMs playing an iterated prisoner's dilemma across ten payoff scales and five languages (English, French, Vietnamese, Chinese and Arabic). 我们使用五个被广泛采用的商业LLM在十个回报量表和五种语言（英语、法语、越南语、中文和阿拉伯语）中迭代囚犯困境来测试这一预测。

Contrary to theoretical expectations, changing only the numerical scale of payoffs substantially alters cooperation. 与理论预期相反，仅改变回报的数字规模会大大改变合作。

Models respond differently, often in opposite directions, meaning that averages across models can conceal important behavioural heterogeneity. 模型的反应不同，通常是相反的方向，这意味着模型之间的平均值可以隐藏重要的行为异质性。

Sensitivity to payoff scale also depends strongly on prompt language. 对回报量表的敏感度也在很大程度上取决于提示性语言。

Moreover, payoff scale modifies the effects of persona instructions: at sub-unit stakes, where no payoff shown exceeds 1, all five models cooperate more under a selfish than a cooperative persona, whereas Claude and Gemini reverse this pattern at larger scales. 此外，回报量表改变了角色指令的效果：在没有显示回报超过1的子单位赌注中，所有五个模型在自私而不是合作角色下合作，而Claude和Gemini在更大规模上扭转了这种模式。

These findings show that cooperation measures depend critically on payoff scale and language and should not be treated as fixed model traits. 这些研究结果表明，合作措施严重依赖于回报规模和语言，不应被视为固定的模型特征。

We introduce StoreBench, a live-commerce environment in which an agent runs a mid-size online apparel store on a production-grade commerce backend, testing long-horizon planning and economic judgment under uncertainty. 我们引入了StoreBench ，这是一个实时商务环境，代理商在生产级商务后端运行中型在线服装商店，在不确定性下测试长远规划和经济判断。

A3 Training LLM Agents to Automate LLM Post-Training with RSI-Dojo

arXiv: 2611.03698 QwenRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) aims to enable AI systems to autonomously build and improve AI systems themselves, including models, harnesses, and underlying infrastructure. 递归自我改进（ RSI ）旨在使人工智能系统能够自主构建和改进人工智能系统本身，包括模型、线束和底层基础设施。

Within this broader agenda, a central capability is enabling agents to train and improve base models. 在这个更广泛的议程中，一个核心能力是使代理能够训练和改进基础模型。

However, existing studies on automated model training primarily focused on workflow- or harness-level optimizations, rather than internalizing the ability to conduct post-training into the model itself. 然而，现有的自动化模型训练研究主要集中在工作流或线束级优化上，而不是将进行后训练的能力内化到模型本身。

Developing this capability requires executable model training environments that can produce high-quality trajectories demonstrating how to select data, train models, diagnose failures, and iteratively improve results. 开发这种能力需要可执行的模型训练环境，这些环境可以生成高质量的轨迹，演示如何选择数据、训练模型、诊断故障以及迭代改进结果。

In this paper, we introduce RSI-Dojo, a collection of executable model training environments designed to teach LLM agents to conduct post-training. 在本文中，我们介绍了RSI-Dojo ，这是一个可执行模型培训环境的集合，旨在教导LLM代理进行后期培训。

To construct RSI-Dojo, we develop RSI-Craft, an automatic pipeline that converts human research artifacts into executable model training environments. 为了构建RSI-Dojo ，我们开发了RSI-Craft ，这是一个将人类研究工件转换为可执行模型训练环境的自动管道。

RSI-Craft automatically collects research papers from top venues, instantiates datasets and metrics into a unified interface, and reproduces evaluation dependencies. RSI-Craft自动收集来自顶级场所的研究论文，将数据集和指标实例化到一个统一的界面中，并重现评估依赖关系。

It then filters tasks by data size, execution reliability, and discriminability, yielding RSI-Dojo with 281 seed model training tasks. 然后，它通过数据大小、执行可靠性和可识别性来过滤任务，产生具有281个种子模型训练任务的RSI-Dojo。

Building on RSI-Dojo, we employ a trajectory collection method with an improved harness to collect high-quality model-training trajectories from frontier models, then retain examples through rubric-based and outcome-based filtering. 在RSI-Dojo的基础上，我们采用带有改进线束的轨迹收集方法，从前沿模型中收集高质量的模型训练轨迹，然后通过基于标题和基于结果的过滤保留示例。

We fine-tune Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on these trajectories. 我们在这些轨迹上微调Qwen3.5-9B和Qwen3.6-35B-A3B。

The resulting RSI-Dojo-9B and RSI-Dojo-35B-A3B improve over their base models by 8.74 and 16.87 PostTrainBench points, respectively. 由此产生的RSI-Dojo-9B和RSI-Dojo-35B-A3B分别比其基础模型提高了8.74和16.87个PostTrainBench点。

RSI-Dojo-35B-A3B reaches 29.95 on PostTrainBench and 53.29 on PostTrainBench-Lite, matching the performance of frontier models such as GLM-5.2 and GPT 5.5. RSI-Dojo-35B-A3B在PostTrainBench上达到29.95 ，在PostTrainBench-Lite上达到53.29 ，与GLM-5.2和GPT 5.5等前沿型号的性能相匹配。

A4 Recursa: Toward Recursively Self-Improving Foundation Model Systems

arXiv: 2611.04035 DeepSeekQwenRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Foundation models in changing environments can encounter unfamiliar domain knowledge that their original training did not cover. 在不断变化的环境中，基础模型可能会遇到原始培训未涵盖的陌生领域知识。

However, new domains rarely provide large amounts of expert-created training data or reliable supervisory signals for further training. 然而，新领域很少为进一步培训提供大量专家创建的培训数据或可靠的监督信号。

We introduce Recursa, a foundation model system that adapts to new domains by learning from its own experience without human experts or a stronger teacher model. 我们引入了Recursa ，这是一个基础模型系统，通过在没有人类专家或更强大的教师模型的情况下学习自己的经验来适应新的领域。

Recursa uses a knowledge repository to store what it learns: domain-specific skills and instructions on when to apply them, along with memories of past task experiences and reflections on what to improve. Recursa使用知识库来存储所学知识：特定领域的技能和何时应用它们的说明，以及过去任务经验的记忆和关于改进的思考。

Recursa bootstraps the repository from domain materials and then refines it through recursive self-improvement. Recursa从域材料引导存储库，然后通过递归自我改进对其进行细化。

It uses parallel learners to execute tasks and synthesize local improvements, then validates and merges the improvements into the knowledge repository. 它使用并行学习者来执行任务并合成局部改进，然后验证改进并将其合并到知识库中。

A5 Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention

arXiv: 2610.42147 QwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Agentic workflows are increasingly shifting towards long-horizon tasks, such as open-ended scientific work, where models benefit from long context windows. 代理工作流程越来越多地转向长期任务，例如开放式科学工作，其中模型受益于长时间的上下文窗口。

However, at ultra-long context (above 1M tokens), full attention is prohibitively expensive. 然而，在超长背景下（超过100万个代币） ，全神贯注的成本过于昂贵。

A promising approach is sparse attention, in which a dedicated indexer selects a subset of keys for the attention. 一种有前途的方法是稀疏注意力，其中专用的索引器选择一个关键的子集来引起注意。

Yet current methods of indexing either still have quadratic cost in context size or lose accuracy at ultra-long context scale. 然而，目前的索引方法要么在上下文大小上仍具有二次代价，要么在超长上下文尺度上失去准确性。

We address this with Latent Sparse Attention (LSA), an efficient attention method that maintains accuracy at ultra-long context. 我们使用潜在稀疏注意（ LSA ）来解决这个问题，这是一种在超长语境下保持准确性的有效注意方法。

In LSA, keys are hierarchically clustered in a learned latent space, and queries score the hierarchy of clusters instead of scoring every key, making prefill, decode and training faster at long context. 在LSA中，密钥在学习的潜在空间中分层聚类，查询对簇的层次结构进行评分，而不是对每个密钥进行评分，从而在长上下文中更快地进行预填充、解码和训练。

We propose two variants, Distilled-LSA (D-LSA), which can be applied to pre-trained models via distillation, and Native-LSA (N-LSA), which is trained natively on the next-token loss alone. 我们提出了两种变体，即蒸馏LSA （ D-LSA ） ，它可以通过蒸馏应用于预训练模型，以及Native-LSA （ N-LSA ） ，它仅在next-token损失上进行本地训练。

On GLM-5.3-FLASH (QWEN-3.8-27B) at 10M tokens, D-LSA speeds up one attention layer by 22.7x (72.4x) in prefill and 4.6x (42.5x) in decode over its original sparse attention (full attention), while keeping similar accuracy across a variety of tasks. 在10M代币的GLM-5.3-FLASH （ QWEN-3.8-27B ）上， D-LSA在其原始稀疏注意（全注意力）的基础上，将一个注意力层的预填充速度提高了22.7倍（ 72.4倍） ，解码速度提高了4.6倍（ 42.5倍） ，同时在各种任务中保持相似的准确性。

N-LSA matches the accuracy of full attention with a training speedup of 1.6x per layer at 128k tokens. N-LSA将全注意力的准确性与128k代币每层1.6倍的训练加速相匹配。

LSA offers practical and efficient attention for models to benefit from ultra-long context. LSA为模型提供实用和高效的关注，以从超长上下文中受益。

A6 ALBI: LLM-Guided Inductive Logic Programming via Iterative Generate-Test-Constrain Refineme

arXiv: 2611.05039 AnthropicGoogleAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

We introduce ALBI (Agent for Learning By Induction), a neuro-symbolic ILP framework that replaces the ASP-based hypothesis generator in the Learning from Failures (LFF) paradigm with a Large Language Model (LLM), while retaining SWI-Prolog as a formal verifier. 我们引入了ALBI （ Agent for Learning By Induction ） ，这是一个神经符号ILP框架，用大型语言模型（ LLM ）取代了从失败中学习（ LFF ）范式中基于ASP的假设生成器，同时保留SWI-Prolog作为正式验证者。

ALBI implements an iterative generate-test-constrain loop: the LLM proposes candidate hypotheses from structured prompts; Prolog tests each candidate; and diagnostic feedback drives refinement. ALBI实现了迭代生成-测试-约束循环： LLM从结构化提示中提出候选人假设； Prolog测试每个候选人；诊断反馈推动细化。

arXiv: 2611.04473 AnthropicAgentLLM

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Scientific agents can find interventions that work without understanding why they do.

We study this failure mode in controlled synthetic regulatory systems, where agents must experimentally identify a hidden fault and rescue the resulting phenotype.

Across seven LLMs, every model restores the healthy phenotypes more often than it identifies the fault.

Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 rescue every organism while diagnosing only 55% and 35% of them.

This failure is not simply due to insufficient exploration or unavailable evidence.

In most cases, the cause is experimentally identifiable, and agents encounter evidence that distinguishes competing causes in 91% of relevant sessions, and still frequently misdiagnose the system.

Targeted probes reveal where experimental reasoning breaks down.

Models reliably interpret decisive evidence and revise hypotheses when the causal comparison is explicit, and can locate that evidence within a full experimental record.

However, they struggle to predict the outcome of the experiment under each competing cause, a necessary step in using the observed result to distinguish between them.

Agents can therefore gather the right evidence and still infer the wrong cause.

As AI systems take on a growing role in synthesising scientific evidence, these findings highlight the need to evaluate how they use evidence to reach causal conclusions, alongside whether their interventions succeed.

A21 Multi-layer Indexer: Overlapping Selection and Attention in DeepSeek Sparse Attention

arXiv: 2611.03768 DeepSeekLLM

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Sparse attention cuts the cost of long-context inference by making the attention matrix sparse: each query attends only a subset of the sequence, chosen by a fixed pattern or, in recent production LLMs, learned per query.

In DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), the attention of DeepSeek-V3.2 and the GLM-5 family, every layer is preceded by a lightning indexer that scores the whole cache and keeps the k=2048 highest-scoring tokens for the layer to attend.

At long context this indexer scan, not the sparse attention it feeds, is where the decode time goes.

But adjacent layers select nearly the same tokens, so a layer can attend the selection of the previous indexer layer instead of its own.

This removes the dependency: each indexer then runs concurrently with the attention of its layer.

The resulting Multi-layer Indexer (MLI) needs no retraining and no new weights.

Its default realization changes no kernel, running inside SGLang's production decode graph; an optional fused decode kernel accelerates it further on DeepSeek-V3.2.

MLI composes with IndexCache (the cross-layer reuse GLM-5.2 ships as IndexShare): IndexCache drops the indexer from some layers, and MLI hides the scan on the rest.

We serve DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5 under IndexCache's protocol at retentions 1/2 to 1/32 (the fraction of layers that keep an indexer) and contexts 10K to 195K.

At equal retention the composition decodes faster than IndexCache: up to 14.5% faster on DeepSeek-V3.2 and 9.8% on GLM-5 at 1/2, and 7.9% and 5.1% at 1/4, largest where IndexCache keeps the most indexers and growing with context.

At retention 1/r it is faster still than IndexCache at 1/(2r) while keeping twice the fresh selections: at 128K it reaches 1.32x the unmodified model's decode throughput at 1/4, above IndexCache's 1.28x at 1/8.

Its LongBench, RULER and LongBench v2 accuracy stays within a few percentage points of IndexCache's.

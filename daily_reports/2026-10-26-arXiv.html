A1 RSI-Opt: Recursive Self-Improvement Optimizer Agents For Model Training

arXiv: 2610.32026 OpenAIRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Conventional neural-network training typically follows a predefined optimization recipe, motivating a LLM agent that monitors training dynamics and adjusts optimizer settings as training progresses, but a fixed agent policy does not accumulate experience across runs. 传统的神经网络培训通常遵循预定义的优化方案，激励LLM代理监控培训动态并随着培训的进展调整优化器设置，但固定代理策略不会在整个运行过程中积累经验。

We introduce RSI-Opt, a plug-and-play Recursive Self-Improvement Optimizer Agents which evolves the knowledge that guides this process: a prompt, a playbook of procedures, a library of tested optimizer code, and a decision cadence. 我们引入了RSI-Opt ，这是一种即插即用的递归自我改进优化代理，它演变了指导此过程的知识：提示、过程剧本、经过测试的优化器代码库和决策节奏。

A meta-agent rewrites this policy using evidence from earlier runs, and an archive retains evaluated policies for further evolution. 元代理使用早期运行的证据重写此策略，存档保留评估的策略以供进一步演进。

Across 5 generations on three training tasks, the meta-agents develop reusable procedures and code and conduct their own CPU proxy experiments. 在三个训练任务的5代中，元代理开发可重用的过程和代码，并进行自己的CPU代理实验。

RSI-Opt substantially improves optimizer-agent performance, with representative raw-space summary $z$-scores increasing from $+10.48$ to $+14.33$ on the evolution suite with Opus 5.5 and from $-0.36$ to $+3.11$ on held-out tasks with GPT-5.5 through cross-model policy transfer. RSI-Opt大大提高了优化器-代理的性能，具有代表性的原始空间摘要$ z $ -分数在带有Opus 5.5的进化套件中从$ +10.48 $增加到$ +14.33 $ ，在带有GPT-5.5的保留任务中通过跨模型策略转移从$ -0.36 $增加到$ +3.11 $。

On the Burgers PDE, it reduces mean relative $L_2$ error by approximately 2.7-fold with Opus 5.5 and 8.8-fold with GPT-6.1 over the fixed-prompt agent baseline, although PDE transfer varies across LLMs and tasks. 在Burgers PDE上，使用Opus 5.5 ，平均相对$ L_2 $误差减少约2.7倍，使用GPT-6.1 ，平均相对相对误差减少约8.8倍，尽管PDE转移因LLM和任务而异。

Controlled comparisons show that the evolved policy combines reusable optimization knowledge with adaptation to each task: initial decisions capture most observed gains on seen tasks, while continued adaptation improves outcomes on unseen tasks. 受控比较表明，演化后的策略将可重用的优化知识与对每个任务的适应性相结合：初始决策捕获了所见任务中观察到的最多收益，而持续的适应性则改善了未见任务的结果。

These findings demonstrate how procedures and executable tools accumulated across training runs can improve optimizer-agent performance and support transfer to scientific machine learning. 这些研究结果表明，在培训运行中积累的程序和可执行工具如何提高优化器代理的性能，并支持向科学机器学习的转移。

A2 TGR-EvoDAG: A DAG-based Multi-Agent Code Evolution Framework for Generative Recommendation on Tencent

arXiv: 2610.32326 HunyuanRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Large Language Model (LLM) agents have enabled automated code evolution for algorithm discovery. 大型语言模型（ LLM ）代理已经为算法发现启用了自动代码演化。

However, current recursive self-improvement (RSI) research for sequential recommendation faces a dilemma: general-purpose frameworks lack domain-specific feedback, while recommender-specific frameworks rely on linear evolution. 然而，目前针对顺序推荐的递归自我改进（ RSI ）研究面临着一个困境：通用框架缺乏特定领域的反馈，而推荐者特定的框架依赖于线性进化。

Crucially, naively integrating them fails when applied to Generative Recommendation (GR). 至关重要的是，当应用于生成式推荐（ GR ）时，天真地整合它们是失败的。

Unlike end-to-end models, GR relies on a module-cascaded pipeline (tokenization, pre-training, and alignment). 与端到端模型不同， GR依赖于模块级联管道（标记化、预训练和对齐）。

In this paradigm, existing evolution methods suffer from: i) cost blindness, squandering budgets by ignoring stage-asymmetric retraining overheads; and ii) short-sighted evaluation, discarding promising upstream innovations that temporarily regress metrics before downstream adaptation. 在这种范式中，现有的演进方法受到以下问题的困扰： i ）成本盲目性，通过忽略阶段性非对称再培训开销而浪费预算;和ii ）短视的评估，放弃有前途的上游创新，这些创新在下游适应之前暂时退化指标。

To bridge these gaps, we propose TGR-EvoDAG, a multi-agent code evolution framework tailored for GR. 为了弥合这些差距，我们提出了TGR-EvoDAG ，这是一个为GR量身定制的多智能体代码演化框架。

TGR-EvoDAG models code evolution as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) to maintain multiple evolution branches and merge complementary innovations, unifying advanced search with rich GR diagnostic feedback. TGR-EvoDAG将代码进化建模为有向无环图（ DAG ） ，以维护多个进化分支并合并互补创新，将高级搜索与丰富的GR诊断反馈统一起来。

We further introduce stage-aware cost planning to optimize GPU-hour budgets, and active downstream co-adaptation to navigate temporary regressions and unlock cross-stage gains. 我们进一步引入了阶段感知成本规划，以优化GPU小时预算，以及积极的下游共同适应，以应对临时回归并解锁跨阶段收益。

On public Amazon datasets, TGR-EvoDAG enables the first autonomous evolution of a complete GR pipeline, improving offline metrics by up to 37.4% and Agent4Rec simulated user satisfaction by 19.7% over the strongest baseline. 在公共Amazon数据集上， TGR-EvoDAG实现了完整GR管道的首次自主演进，使离线指标提高了37.4% ， Agent4Rec模拟用户满意度比最强基线提高了19.7%。

Furthermore, in industrial deployment on Tencent's large-scale commercial media platform, the evolved GR achieves a 12.16% relative improvement in offline Hit@1 and a statistically significant 0.40% increase in online click-through rate (CTR). 此外，在腾讯大型商业媒体平台的行业部署中，演进后的GR在线下点击率@ 1上实现了12.16%的相对提升，在线点击率（ CTR ）上实现了0.40%的统计显著提升。

A3 HarnessMM: Recursive Self-Improvement for Harness Token Efficiency

arXiv: 2610.32813 DeepSeekRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Agent harnesses repeatedly invoke reasoning after tool feedback, even when an earlier call has already fixed the next action. 客服代表在工具反馈后反复调用推理，即使之前的呼叫已经修复了下一个操作。

We introduce HarnessMM (Harness Minus Minus), a method for improving harness token efficiency through guarded decision reuse and recursive self-improvement (RSI). 我们引入了HarnessMM （ Harness Minus Minus ） ，这是一种通过保护决策重用和递归自我改进（ RSI ）来提高利用令牌效率的方法。

RSI iteratively refines companion harness configurations from training feedback, with validation selecting the configuration for deployment. RSI从培训反馈中迭代优化配套线束配置，并通过验证选择要部署的配置。

A controller at the model-tool boundary reuses resolved decisions to replace later reasoning callbacks while actor weights remain fixed. 模型-工具边界的控制器重用已解析的决策来替换后来的推理回调，而参与者权重保持不变。

Executable Decision Graphs store exact actions, literal arguments, causal dependencies, and public guards. 可执行决策图存储精确操作、字面参数、因果依赖关系和公共防护。

Guarded Decision Reuse activates these persistent decisions from observed receipts; Certified Callback Substitution executes them through the native interface and records removal of the registered slow callback. Guarded Decision Reuse从观察到的回执中激活这些持续的决策； Certified Callback Substitution通过本机界面执行这些决策，并记录已注册的慢速回调的删除。

New semantic decisions, unsupported evidence, and selector abstention invoke the original actor again. 新的语义决策、不受支持的证据和选择器弃权会再次调用原始参与者。

The representation restricts what feedback may change, while complete-task measurements establish quality and cost. 表示形式限制了反馈可能发生的变化，而完整的任务测量则确定了质量和成本。

On HumanEval, HarnessMM preserves Codex success at 90.82\% while reducing Tokens/task (k) from 66.72 to 47.02 (29.54\% saving). 在HumanEval上， HarnessMM将Codex的成功率保持在90.82\ % ，同时将代币/任务（ k ）从66.72降低到47.02 （节省29.54\ % ）。

For DeepSeek Harness, success rises from 71.43\% to 85.71\% while Tokens/task (k) fall from 96.28 to 11.62 (87.93\% saving). 对于DeepSeek线束，成功率从71.43\ %上升到85.71\ % ，而代币/任务（ k ）从96.28下降到11.62 （节省87.93\ % ）。

A separate MBPP ablation measures 26.91\% lower cost than single-use graphs and 19.52\% lower cost than retained callbacks. 单独的MBPP消融比一次性使用图表成本低26.91\ % ，比保留的回拨成本低19.52\ %。

These results establish guarded decision reuse as a practical way to reduce repeated reasoning in existing harnesses. 这些结果确立了谨慎决策重用作为减少现有线束中重复推理的实用方法。

A4 An Agentic Approach with Evidence-Grounded Verification for Multimodal Emotion Reasoning in Conversation

arXiv: 2610.31685 MetaQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

LLM-based multimodal emotion reasoning systems perform well on standard benchmarks, but improving them through instruction tuning can be computationally expensive. 基于LLM的多模态情感推理系统在标准基准上表现良好，但通过指令调整来改进它们的计算成本可能很高。

Agentic pipelines built from frozen models avoid this cost, but introduce a different challenge: when a prediction is wrong, the system must identify which agent or stage caused the error. 基于冻结模型构建的代理管道避免了这一成本，但引入了一个不同的挑战：当预测错误时，系统必须确定导致错误的代理或阶段。

We address this problem with an agentic framework for traceable multimodal emotion reasoning and prompt-space adaptation. 我们通过可追溯的多模态情感推理和即时空间适应的代理框架来解决这个问题。

Modality-specific perception agents analyze visual, acoustic, and lexical inputs and post evidence-cited observations to a shared board. 模态特定的感知代理分析视觉、声学和词汇输入，并将证据引用的观察结果发布到共享板。

An integration agent combines these observations with retrieved multimodal examples to produce an emotion description, label set, and supporting citations, while an MLLM critic can request one revision before the output is finalized. 集成代理将这些观察结果与检索到的多模态示例相结合，以生成情感描述、标签集和支持引用，而MLLM批评者可以在输出最终确定之前请求一个修订。

For incorrect predictions, a separate evidence-grounded verification stage applies deterministic checks to verify that cited evidence and references are valid, and an entailment check to determine whether the final claims are supported by that evidence. 对于不正确的预测，一个单独的基于证据的验证阶段应用确定性检查来验证引用的证据和参考文献是否有效，并进行牵连检查以确定最终索赔是否得到该证据的支持。

Detected failures are then grouped by the responsible component and failure type, converted into targeted prompt patches, and accepted only after validation on a held-out set. 然后，检测到的故障按负责组件和故障类型分组，转换为有针对性的提示修补程序，并仅在保留集上进行验证后才接受。

This adapts the agent prompts without updating model weights. 这将在不更新模型权重的情况下调整代理提示。

On the MER2025 dataset, targeted prompt adaptation outperforms the frozen static pipeline on all four MLLMs, ranging from 2B to 38B parameters, and matches or outperforms undifferentiated reflection baselines on each of them: VideoLLaMA3-2B, Phi-3.5-vision-instruct, Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct, and InternVL2.5-38B. 在MER2025数据集上，有针对性的提示适应在所有四个MLLM （范围从2B到38B参数）上都优于冻结的静态管线，并且在每个MLLM上匹配或优于未分化的反射基线： VideoLLaMA3-2B、Phi-3.5-vision-instruct、Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct和InternVL2.5-38B。

It improves F-score by 0.6--2.2 points depending on the backbone, including an increase from 52.3 to 54.5 on Qwen2.5-VL-7B. 它根据骨干将F分数提高了0.6-2.2分，包括在Qwen2.5-VL-7B上从52.3分提高到54.5分。

A5 Compact-Bench: Measuring What Context Compaction Costs Long-Horizon Coding Agents

arXiv: 2610.31710 AnthropicDeepSeekKimi

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Context compaction lets long-horizon coding agents continue beyond their context windows, but can erase the state needed to finish a task. 上下文压缩允许长距离编码代理继续超出其上下文窗口，但可以擦除完成任务所需的状态。

Prior evaluations measure efficiency, retained information, or end-to-end success, leaving the effect of a specific history replacement difficult to isolate. 先前的评估衡量效率、保留的信息或端到端的成功，使特定历史记录替换的影响难以隔离。

Compact-Bench pairs full-history and summary-only continuations from a shared repository state, and evaluates recurring details and successive production compactions. Compact-Bench将共享存储库状态的完整历史记录和仅总结的延续配对，并评估重复的详细信息和连续的生产压缩。

It covers 35 SWE-bench repairs, 146 agentic sessions, and 106 production chains. 它涵盖35个SWE-bench维修， 146个代理会话和106个生产链。

Across 14 summarizer backbones, stable damage ranges from 5-64% of 22 full-history successes: Claude Opus 4.8 preserves the most repairs, while DeepSeek-Flash damages the most. 在14个汇总器主干中，稳定的损坏范围在22个全历史成功案例的5-64%之间： Claude Opus 4.8保留了最多的修复，而DeepSeek-Flash损坏最多。

With Kimi-K3 as summarizer, the Kimi Code prompt preserves the most task continuations (19/22), whereas Claude Code retains the most recurring details (15.0%). 以Kimi-K3为总结者， Kimi Code提示保留了最多的任务延续（ 19/22 ） ，而Claude Code保留了最常见的细节（ 15.0% ）。

LLM summaries retain at most 16.0% of recurring details, compared with 79.1% for mechanical extraction; production summaries retain 15.7% at the first boundary. LLM总结最多保留16.0 ％的经常性细节，而机械提取为79.1 ％ ；生产总结保留第一个边界的15.7 ％。

Failures recur across methods: one repair is damaged by 13/14 backbones, and another by all 5 prompts. 故障在各种方法中重复发生：一次修复被13/14骨架损坏，另一次被全部5个提示损坏。

These results show that reliable compaction requires preserving the progress needed to complete a task, and that retaining relevant information alone does not ensure successful continuation. 这些结果表明，可靠的压实要求保留完成任务所需的进度，而仅保留相关信息并不能确保成功延续。

Code is available at https://github.com/cai-jianfeng/compact-bench and data is available at https://huggingface.co/datasets/caijanfeng/compact-bench. 代码可在https://github.com/cai-jianfeng/compact-bench上获得，数据可在https://huggingface.co/datasets/caijanfeng/compact-bench上获得。

A6 Tool-Calling LLM Agents Under Realistic MCP Faults: Scale Does Not Purchase Fault Tolerance

arXiv: 2610.32555 OpenAIAgentLLM

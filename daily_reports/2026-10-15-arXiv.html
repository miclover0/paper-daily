A1 Video-Browser: Towards Open-web Agentic Video Browsing

arXiv: 2512.23044 GoogleQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Autonomous agents are shifting information seeking from passive retrieval toward proactive, open-ended research, yet this progress has largely bypassed video, one of the web's richest sources of evidence. 自主代理正在将寻求的信息从被动检索转向主动、开放式的研究，但这一进展在很大程度上绕过了视频，视频是网络上最丰富的证据来源之一。

We formalize Agentic Video Browsing, in which an agent must query video search engines, decide which returned videos to watch, and answer questions grounded in their visual content. 我们将Agentic Video Browsing正式化，在Agentic Video Browsing中，客服代表必须查询视频搜索引擎，决定要观看哪些返回的视频，并回答基于其视觉内容的问题。

To study this setting reproducibly, we introduce Video-BrowseComp, a benchmark and infrastructure suite with 660 human-annotated questions at three difficulty levels, paired online and offline evaluation tracks, and a fixed video search stack of roughly 12,000 videos spanning over 2,000 hours for evaluation and training-data construction. 为了可重复地研究此设置，我们引入了Video-BrowseComp ，这是一个基准和基础设施套件，包含三个难度级别的660个人工注释问题，配对在线和离线评估轨道，以及大约12,000个视频的固定视频搜索堆栈，跨越2,000多个小时，用于评估和培训数据构建。

Our analysis reveals that the main bottleneck lies before video understanding: agents often fail to find the answer-bearing video at all. 我们的分析表明，主要的瓶颈在于视频理解：客服代表往往根本无法找到含有答案的视频。

This bottleneck stems from two coupled challenges. 这一瓶颈源于两个相互关联的挑战。

Search engines index textual metadata such as titles and tags, whereas questions often target implicit visual or cross-source evidence absent from these surface forms; and exhaustively inspecting the many returned videos is prohibitively expensive. 搜索引擎索引标题和标签等文本元数据，而问题通常针对这些表面形式中缺少的隐含视觉或跨源证据；彻底审查许多返回的视频是非常昂贵的。

We propose Video-Browser, an evidence-surfacing agent that improves both sides of search. 我们推荐Video-Browser ，这是一种证据展示代理，可改善搜索的两面性。

A Translation Memory helps the Planner phrase queries by analogy to past question-to-query transformations that successfully surfaced their target videos, and a Watcher performs sparse visual screening over returned candidates, retaining only videos that plausibly contain answer-bearing evidence for later dense verification. 翻译记忆库通过类比过去成功呈现其目标视频的问答转换来帮助规划师进行短语查询，而观察者对返回的候选人进行稀疏的视觉筛选，仅保留可能包含答案证据的视频，以便日后进行密集验证。

On the offline track, Video-Browser raises overall accuracy from 11.6% to 27.3% with Qwen3-VL-8B and from 39.8% to 45.1% with Gemini-3.1-Pro over the corresponding ReAct search agents, showing that better query formulation and post-search candidate selection improve end-to-end browsing performance. 在离线轨道上， Video-Browser使用Qwen3-VL-8B将整体准确率从11.6%提高到27.3% ，使用Gemini-3.1-Pro将整体准确率从39.8%提高到45.1% ，优于相应的ReAct搜索代理，这表明更好的查询公式和搜索后候选选择提高了端到端的浏览性能。

A2 Verifier-Grounded Agentic RL for Provably Correct Code

arXiv: 2610.19298 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Reinforcement learning for code usually rewards passing tests, which a model can do while remaining wrong on the inputs the tests do not cover. 代码的强化学习通常会奖励通过测试，模型可以在测试未覆盖的输入上保持错误的情况下执行此操作。

A program verifier instead decides whether a program satisfies a formal specification for all inputs. 相反，程序验证器决定程序是否满足所有输入的形式规范。

Language models are almost always paired with verifiers at inference time only, and turning a verifier into a reward raises two difficulties. 语言模型几乎总是仅在推理时与验证器配对，而将验证器转化为奖励会带来两个困难。

It reports only acceptance or rejection, which gives no gradient when every attempt fails, and a policy can cheat it, most simply by telling it to assume what the proof was supposed to establish. 它只报告接受或拒绝，每次尝试失败时都不会给出梯度，政策可以欺骗它，最简单的方法是让它假设证据应该建立什么。

We build an agentic environment and reward that address both. 我们构建了一个代理环境，并奖励这两者。

The reward reads the verifier's diagnostics rather than its verdict, scoring a candidate by the weighted fraction of proof obligations it discharges, extended with hidden probes from a reference proof's annotations. 奖励读取验证者的诊断而不是其判决，根据其履行的证明义务的加权分数对候选者进行评分，并从参考证明的注释中扩展隐藏探针。

An integrity audit outranks the verifier, rejecting any candidate that alters the specification or assumes what it was meant to prove. 诚信审计的排名高于验证者，拒绝任何改变规范或假设其目的是证明的候选人。

The model works as an agent against a live verifier over many turns, with a separately trained proof specialist as a frozen collaborator. 该模型在多个回合中作为代理与实时验证者对抗，并由经过单独培训的证明专家作为冻结的合作者。

Across Qwen3 models from 1.7B to 14B and a 35B Qwen3.6 mixture-of-experts model, held-out verification on code generation rises from 66.1% to 93.5% on DafnyBench and from 56.3% to 100% on SWE-Proof. 在从1.7B到14B的Qwen3模型和35B Qwen3.6混合专家模型中， DafnyBench的代码生成验证从66.1%上升到93.5% ， SWE-Proof从56.3%上升到100%。

This reward leads every other training signal we compare, including a binary pass-or-fail reward trained under otherwise identical settings, with the widest margin on code generation. 这种奖励领先于我们比较的所有其他训练信号，包括在其他相同设置下训练的二进制通过或失败奖励，并且在代码生成上的余量最大。

When the agent must also deliver the repository patch, graded by each project's withheld tests, the fraction of held-out issues it resolves rises from 59.6% to 72.5%. 当代理还必须提供存储库补丁时，根据每个项目的扣留测试进行分级，其解决的扣留问题的比例从59.6%上升到72.5%。

Evaluated unchanged on three verification suites withheld from training, the checkpoints improve on all of them, including one whose language and verifier are absent from training. 对三个未接受培训的验证套件进行评估后，所有检查点都得到了改善，其中包括一个语言和验证者缺席培训的检查点。

A3 SWE-Spot: Building Small Repo-Experts with Repository-Centric Learning

arXiv: 2601.21649 OpenAIQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

The deployment of coding agents in privacy-sensitive and resource-constrained environments drives the demand for capable open-weight Small Language Models (SLMs). 在隐私敏感和资源受限的环境中部署编码代理，推动了对功能强大的开放式小型语言模型(SLM)的需求。

However, they suffer from a fundamental capability gap: unlike frontier large models, they lack the inference-time strong generalization to handle complicated, unfamiliar codebases. 然而，它们存在根本的能力差距：与前沿大型模型不同，它们缺乏处理复杂、不熟悉的代码库的推理时间强泛化。

We identify that the prevailing Task-Centric Learning (TCL) approach, which scales exposure across disparate repositories, fails to address this limitation. 我们发现，主流的以任务为中心的学习（ TCL ）方法未能解决这一局限性，该方法在不同的存储库中扩展暴露。

In response, we propose Repository-Centric Learning (RCL), a shift that prioritizes vertical, single-repository depth over horizontal, single-task breadth for SLM training. 作为回应，我们提出了以存储库为中心的学习（ RCL ） ，这种转变优先考虑了SLM培训的垂直、单存储库深度而不是水平、单任务广度。

To accomplish this depth, we design a four-unit Repository-Centric Experience, transforming static codebases into interactive learning signals, to train SWE-Spot-4B, a family of highly compact models built as repo-specialized experts. 为了实现这一深度，我们设计了一个由四个单元组成的以存储库为中心的体验，将静态代码库转换为交互式学习信号，以培训SWE-Spot-4B ，这是一个高度紧凑的模型系列，作为存储库专业化专家构建。

Under controlled comparisons with the same teacher and data budget, RCL consistently outperforms TCL across tasks, teachers, model scales, and programming languages. 在同一教师和数据预算的受控比较下， RCL在任务、教师、模型量表和编程语言方面始终优于TCL。

On seven target repositories, SWE-Spot-4B experts match or exceed existing open-weight models up to $8\times$ larger (e.g., CWM by Meta, Qwen3-Coder-30B) and surpass or match efficiency-focused commercial models (e.g., GPT-4.1-mini, GPT-5-nano) across multiple SWE tasks. 在七个目标存储库中， SWE-Spot-4B专家匹配或超过现有的开放权重模型，最高可达$ 8\ times $ （例如， Meta的CWM ， Qwen3-Coder-30B ） ，并在多个SWE任务中超过或匹配以效率为重点的商业模型（例如， GPT-4.1-mini ， GPT-5-nano ）。

Further analysis reveals that RCL yields higher training and inference efficiency, deep knowledge acquisition, and can scale to repository-cluster experts. 进一步分析表明， RCL可以产生更高的培训和推理效率，深度知识获取，并可以扩展到存储库集群专家。

Our work emphasizes that for building efficient intelligence, repository mastery is a distinct, necessary, and practical dimension that complements general coding capability. 我们的工作强调，为了构建高效的智能，存储库掌握是一个独特、必要和实用的维度，是对一般编码能力的补充。

A4 Harness^2: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World

arXiv: 2610.14224 GoogleRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

LLM agents' capabilities depend not only on their underlying models but also on their execution harnesses. LLM代理的能力不仅取决于其基础模型，还取决于其执行利用。

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) offers a promising approach to automatically evolve these harnesses. 递归自我改进（ RSI ）提供了一种有希望的方法来自动发展这些线束。

However, deploying RSI in open-world settings still faces two critical challenges. 然而，在开放世界中部署RSI仍然面临两个关键挑战。

First, open-world tasks are typically unseen during the harness design phase and novel at test time, leaving no ground-truth verifiers to reliably guide online harness edits. 首先，开放世界的任务在线束设计阶段通常是看不到的，在测试时是新颖的，没有留下可靠地指导在线束编辑的地面真相验证器。

Second, the RSI process remains highly task-specific and fails to generalize to novel scenarios. 其次， RSI过程仍然具有高度的任务特定性，无法推广到新颖的场景。

We introduce Harness^2, a plug-and-play, recursive agent harnessing framework that addresses these challenges through two connected levels of recursion. 我们引入了线束^ 2 ，这是一个即插即用的递归代理利用框架，通过两个相互关联的递归级别来解决这些挑战。

Task-level recursion refines the current task's harness using a contrastive Propose-Probe-Compose step that reliably guides edits without ground-truth verifiers, and can be scaled further via parallel or sequential recursion. 任务级递归使用对比的Propose-Probe-Compose步骤来优化当前任务的线束，该步骤在没有地面真实性验证器的情况下可靠地指导编辑，并且可以通过并行或顺序递归进一步缩放。

Harnessing-level recursion then extracts experience from these edits to update a continual improvement harness, enabling the agent to better adapt to future tasks. 利用级别递归然后从这些编辑中提取经验，以更新持续改进利用，使代理能够更好地适应未来的任务。

Across three professional benchmarks and four model-harness configurations, a single step consistently improves GLM-5 and Gemini 3.5 Flash over their base harnesses, yielding gains of up to 11.5 points on WorkBuddy-Bench, 4.5 points on JobBench, and 11.8 percentage points on LAB, with further gains as the task-level recursion budget increases. 在三个专业基准和四个模型线束配置中，单一步骤不断改进GLM-5和Gemini 3.5 Flash的基础线束，在WorkBuddy-Bench上获得高达11.5分的收益，在JobBench上获得4.5分，在实验室获得11.8个百分点，随着任务级递归预算的增加，进一步获得收益。

A5 EvoCowork: A Benchmark for Real-World Office Tasks

arXiv: 2610.14828 RSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Office workflows are a demanding testbed for self-improving agents because their deliverables must be correct, natively editable, and mutually consistent. 对于自我改进的代理而言， Office工作流是一个苛刻的测试平台，因为它们的可交付成果必须正确、本机可编辑且相互一致。

A central question is whether gains on feedback tasks reflect reusable procedures rather than task-specific fixes. 一个核心问题是，反馈任务的收益是否反映了可重复使用的程序，而不是特定于任务的修复。

We introduce EvoCowork, a 240-task benchmark for evaluating harness-level recursive self-improvement across four office modalities, with model weights held fixed. 我们引入了EvoCowork ，这是一个240项任务的基准，用于评估四种办公模式的线束级递归自我改进，模型权重保持不变。

The benchmark covers presentations, documents, spreadsheets, and cross-office workflows, and scores submitted artifacts for content, native structure, formulas, cross-file consistency, and visual quality where needed. 该基准涵盖演示文稿、文档、电子表格和跨部门工作流程，并在需要时对提交的内容、原生结构、公式、交叉文件一致性和视觉质量进行评分。

Its protocol separates feedback, revision selection, and final testing: methods learn from single-modality tasks, then are evaluated on unseen single-modality tasks and cross-office workflows under a shared initial harness and explicit budgets. 其协议将反馈、修订选择和最终测试分开：方法从单模态任务中学习，然后在共享的初始线束和显式预算下根据看不见的单模态任务和跨部门工作流程进行评估。

In static evaluations of two models and four harnesses, the best configuration scores 42.25 overall and 30.62 on cross-office tasks. 在两种型号和四种线束的静态评估中，最佳配置总体得分为42.25 ，跨部门任务得分为30.62。

Starting from the same MSA harness with GLM 5.3, ACE and AHE improve in-domain scores by 4.90 and 2.64 points, respectively, but change cross-office scores by only +0.03 and -1.72. 从与GLM 5.3相同的MSA线束开始， ACE和AHE分别将域内分数提高了4.90和2.64分，但仅将跨部门分数提高了+0.03和-1.72。

Thus, gains on the file types that provide feedback do not necessarily transfer to coordinated workflows. 因此，提供反馈的文件类型的增益不一定会转移到协调的工作流中。

EvoCowork makes this distinction explicit while recording the capability, transfer, and cost of harness evolution. EvoCowork在记录线束演进的能力、转移和成本的同时，明确了这一区别。

Code and task packages: https://github.com/DeepThinkingZhouLiu/cowork-evolution-benchmark. 代码和任务包： https://github.com/DeepThinkingZhouLiu/cowork-evolution-benchmark。

A6 Trace Evolution Bench: Measuring Whether Agents Improve from Their Own Environment Traces

arXiv: 2610.15055 DeepSeekRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Self-evolving agents are usually scored by endpoint Gain: held-out success after an update minus success before. 自我进化药剂通常按终点增益评分：更新后保持成功减去之前的成功。

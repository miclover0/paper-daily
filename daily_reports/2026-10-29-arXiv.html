A1 Rare, Relevant, and Still Wrong: How Memory Fails LLM Group Decisions

arXiv: 2610.37624 MetaDeepSeekQwen

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

When an LLM group's discussion is compressed into a short memory for a final decision maker, what should the memory keep? 当法学硕士小组的讨论被压缩成最终决策者的短暂记忆时，记忆应该保留什么？

Two natural heuristics keep the rarest claims or the claims relevant to the decision criteria. 两种自然启发法保留了最罕见的主张或与决策标准相关的主张。

On hidden-profile tasks both look attractive, because how widely a fact is held is confounded with whether it helps. 在隐藏的个人资料任务上，两者看起来都很有吸引力，因为事实的广度与它是否有帮助相混淆。

We introduce CrossProfile, a diagnostic benchmark of 60 templates with automatically verified answer keys, in which the ownership of supporting, decoy, and irrelevant facts is crossed while their text is held fixed (480 tasks). 我们引入了CrossProfile ，这是一个由60个模板组成的诊断基准，具有自动验证的答案键，其中支持、诱饵和不相关事实的所有权被交叉，而其文本被固定（ 480个任务）。

Across Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, and two DeepSeek models, both heuristics fail in predictable places. 在Qwen2.5-7B、Llama-3.1-8B和两个DeepSeek模型中，两种启发式方法都在可预测的地方失败。

Selecting by relevance readmits decoys, because decoys are on topic: even a perfect relevance filter loses 23 to 31 points to rarest-first selection in the classic hidden-profile configuration. 通过相关性再入选诱饵，因为诱饵是主题：即使是完美的相关性过滤器，在经典的隐藏配置中，也会比最罕见的优先选择损失23到31分。

Rarest-first selection is no better than random selection on average and wins only where the rare facts happen to be the useful ones. 平均而言，最罕见的优先选择并不比随机选择好，只有在罕见的事实恰好是有用的事实时才会获胜。

In both cases accuracy follows the balance of evidence the memory keeps, not the policy that kept it. 在这两种情况下，准确性都取决于记忆保持的证据平衡，而不是保持记忆的政策。

The decision model adds a third failure, a form of social proof: it gives extra weight to facts attributed to many agents. 决策模型增加了第三种失败，这是一种社会证明形式：它对归因于许多代理人的事实给予了额外的重视。

Removing the attribution tags, or reassigning them at random with line order fixed, raises accuracy in the classic configuration by 13 to 24 points. 删除归因标签，或在行顺序固定的情况下随机重新分配归因标签，可将经典配置中的准确率提高13至24分。

In natural discussions the evidence is lost before any memory acts: the complete transcript yields lower accuracy than no notes at all. 在自然讨论中，证据在任何记忆作用之前都会丢失：完整的成绩单的准确性低于完全没有音符的准确性。

A2 Who Convinced Whom? Tracing How LLMs Change Their Minds in Multi-Agent Systems

arXiv: 2610.35930 QwenAgentMulti-Agent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Multi-agent debate (MAD) aims to improve language-model decisions through the exchange of arguments, yet agreement after debate need not reflect independent corroboration. 多代理辩论（ MAD ）旨在通过交换论点来改善语言模型决策，但辩论后的协议无需反映独立的佐证。

We investigate how peer communication influences individual decisions and collective outcomes through behavioral and internal interventions. 我们研究同伴沟通如何通过行为和内部干预影响个人决策和集体结果。

We introduce MAD Bench, a 2,400-question benchmark spanning knowledge and reasoning, values and moral judgment, and safety and refusal, and evaluate three open-weight models using a round-matched independent-reflection control. 我们引入了MAD Bench ，这是一个跨越知识和推理、价值观和道德判断以及安全性和拒绝的2400个问题基准，并使用圆形匹配的独立反射控制来评估三个开放权重模型。

Among answer-changing responses, a forced-choice readout before rationale generation matches the eventual answer more often in debate than in reflection (65.4% versus 22.3% on Qwen3.5-9B). 在改变答案的回答中，在理性生成之前的强制选择读数与最终答案相匹配的辩论频率高于反思（ Qwen3.5-9B为65.4%对22.3% ）。

Message ablations reveal model-dependent effects of peer answers and reasoning, while attributing identical text to the receiver rather than another agent substantially changes its influence. 消息消融揭示了同伴答案和推理的模型依赖性影响，而将相同的文本归因于接收者而不是另一个代理会大大改变其影响。

For the tested source descriptions, stated track record affects answer preferences more strongly than professional-status cues across all three models. 对于测试的源描述，在所有三个模型中，所陈述的跟踪记录对答案偏好的影响比专业状态提示更强烈。

Attention masking produces different changes in decision margins across the three checkpoints. 注意力掩蔽会在三个检查点的决策余量中产生不同的变化。

On fully parsed, reference-labelled non-numeric questions, debate yields small estimated accuracy improvements over initial majority voting, while increasing the rate of unanimous wrong answers by 3.1-7.4 percentage points relative to reflection. 在完全解析的、带有参考标记的非数字问题上，与初始多数投票相比，辩论产生了较小的估计准确性改进，同时相对于反思将一致错误答案的比率提高了3.1-7.4个百分点。

Longer debates show persistent consensus and greater correlation between agents' correctness than reflection. 较长的辩论表明，与反思相比，客服代表的正确性之间存在持久的共识和更大的相关性。

These findings show that stronger consensus can coexist with more shared errors, motivating evaluations that assess dependence and unanimous errors alongside accuracy. 这些研究结果表明，更强的共识可以与更多的共同错误共存，激励评估依赖性和一致性错误以及准确性的评估。

A3 Action-Value Calibration via Continuation Sharing for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning

arXiv: 2610.37195 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Reinforcement learning for long-horizon agents relies on feedback from sequences of interactions, making credit assignment to individual actions difficult. 长期客服代表的强化学习依赖于一系列交互的反馈，因此难以将学分分配给个人操作。

Existing methods such as GiGPO compare actions at shared states to compute relative advantages, but estimate each action's value from a single sampled continuation. 现有的方法（如GiGPO ）比较共享状态下的动作以计算相对优势，但从单个采样连续中估计每个动作的值。

The resulting local credit remains sensitive to decisions made later in the trajectory. 由此产生的本地信贷对后来在轨迹中做出的决定仍然敏感。

To address this problem, we propose ConCal (Continuation Calibration), a recursive continuation-value estimator that incorporates continuation evidence already present in a rollout batch. 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了ConCal （连续校准） ，这是一个递归的连续值估计器，它结合了已经在推出批次中存在的连续证据。

ConCal matches visits from the same task by their incoming state-action-state transitions, combines shared continuation evidence with each visit's own transition estimate, and propagates calibrated values backward to refine earlier action-value estimates. ConCal通过传入的状态-行动-状态转换来匹配来自同一任务的访问，将共享的连续性证据与每次访问自己的转换估计相结合，并向后传播校准值以优化早期的动作值估计。

This reduces dependence on individual sampled continuations and mitigates local credit distortions without additional rollouts. 这减少了对单个抽样连续性的依赖，并减轻了本地信用扭曲，而无需额外推出。

Held-out return diagnostics show lower error in within-group value differences and higher agreement with reference action orderings than estimates based on individual continuations. 与基于个体连续性的估计相比，保留的返回诊断显示组内值差异的误差较小，并且与参考动作排序的一致性较高。

With Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, ConCal improves success rates over GiGPO by 3.39 and 5.86 percentage points on ALFWorld and WebShop, respectively. 通过Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct ， ConCal在ALFWorld和WebShop上的成功率分别比GiGPO提高了3.39和5.86个百分点。

Our code is available at https://github.com/Yingjian-Chen/ConCal. 我们的代码可在https://github.com/Yingjian-Chen/ConCal上找到。

A4 Spine: Compiling Subtask Trees into Structured Context for Long-Horizon Coding Agents

arXiv: 2610.37224 OpenAIGoogleQwen

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Long-horizon coding tasks contain nested subtasks. 长距离编码任务包含嵌套子任务。

Typically, agents record interactions sequentially into a growing history, causing context inflation and performance degradation. 通常，客服代表会按顺序将互动记录到不断增长的历史记录中，从而导致上下文膨胀和绩效下降。

Compressing or trimming the history without explicit subtask boundaries can discard information needed by unfinished subtasks. 在没有明确子任务边界的情况下压缩或修剪历史记录可能会丢弃未完成子任务所需的信息。

Conversely, recursive subagent delegation isolates completed subtasks through child agents by assigning every subtask boundary to delegation, which conflates subtask granularity with executing agent choice, adding execution and coordination overhead. 相反，递归子代理委派通过子代理将每个子任务边界分配给委派来隔离已完成的子任务，这会将子任务粒度与执行代理选择混为一谈，增加了执行和协调开销。

We introduce Spine, an interface and runtime that align model context with subtask nesting and completion. 我们引入了Spine ，这是一个将模型上下文与子任务嵌套和完成对齐的接口和运行时。

An agent declares explicit subtask boundaries through work scopes, allowing the same agent to execute nested subtasks inline while reserving delegation for independent parallel tasks. 代理通过工作范围声明明确的子任务边界，允许同一代理内联执行嵌套子任务，同时为独立的并行任务保留委派。

The runtime organizes execution in SpineTree, a persistent tree recording subtask relations, local interactions, completion states, and returned results. 运行时在SpineTree中组织执行，这是一个记录子任务关系、本地交互、完成状态和返回结果的持久树。

Before each model call, it compiles the tree into model context just in time, retaining unfinished subtasks' interactions and replacing completed subtasks' details with their results, while preserving prefix-cache reuse. 在每个模型调用之前，它会及时将树编译为模型上下文，保留未完成的子任务交互，并将已完成的子任务详细信息替换为其结果，同时保留前缀缓存重用。

We integrate Spine into Codex to build SpineCodex. 我们将Spine集成到Codex中以构建SpineCodex。

With GPT-5.6 Sol, compared with native Codex, SpineCodex improves SWE-Milestone Score by 11.8% while reducing total cost by 32.4%, improves the average ProgramBench test-pass rate by 17.3%, and improves Mean@5 on eight of the ten evaluated FrontierSWE tasks. 使用GPT-5.6 SOL ，与原生Codex相比， SpineCodex将SWE-Milestone得分提高了11.8% ，同时将总成本降低了32.4% ，将ProgramBench的平均测试通过率提高了17.3% ，并在十项评估的FrontierSWE任务中的八项上提高了Mean @ 5。

On SWE-Milestone, it also achieves a comparable Score to Recursive Subagent at 73.7% lower total cost. 在SWE-Milestone上，它还实现了与递归子代理相当的分数，总成本降低了73.7%。

We also integrate Spine into Pi through a reusable software package: with Gemini 3.8 Flash and Qwen 3.8 27B, it improves SWE-Milestone Score by 9.5-17.5% and the average ProgramBench test-pass rate by 4.3-8.7% compared with native Pi. 我们还通过可重用的软件包将Spine集成到Pi中：使用Gemini 3.8 Flash和Qwen 3.8 27B ，与原生Pi相比，它将SWE-Milestone得分提高了9.5-17.5% ，平均ProgramBench测试通过率提高了4.3-8.7%。

A5 HiMA-Ecom: A Long-Horizon Memory Benchmark for Hierarchical Multi-Agent E-commerce Assistants

arXiv: 2506.19846 DeepSeekAgentMulti-Agent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Long-horizon agentic systems become long-context systems by accumulation rather than through a single long document, since every step reassembles a prompt from role instructions, tool and sub-agent descriptions, recalled memories, dialogue history, and previous tool outputs, separately for every agent. 长视野代理系统通过累积而不是通过单个长文档成为长上下文系统，因为每个步骤都分别为每个代理从角色指令、工具和子代理描述、召回记忆、对话历史记录和以前的工具输出中重新组装提示。

This paper studies how such accumulated context can be trained, evaluated, and managed in hierarchical multi-agent assistants. 本文研究如何在分层多智能体助手中训练、评估和管理这种积累的上下文。

We present HiMA-Ecom, a hierarchical multi-agent benchmark for e-commerce assistants with 22.8K instances, of which 17.7K carry memory entries in context, pairing agent-specific supervised fine-tuning data with system-level supervision for end-to-end multi-agent reinforcement learning. 我们展示了HiMA-Ecom ，这是一个针对电子商务助理的分层多Agent基准，有22.8K个实例，其中17.7K实例在上下文中承载内存条目，将特定于Agent的监督微调数据与端到端多Agent强化学习的系统级监督配对。

Building upon it, HiMA-R1 keeps the context budget of a trajectory under control through Variance-Reduction Group Relative Policy Optimization (VR-GRPO), which samples along an initial trajectory so that sampling cost grows additively with trajectory length and updates the agents with the largest reward variance, together with an efficiency reward on trajectory length and a memory evolution mechanism that repurposes GRPO rewards as cost-free signals for what stays in context. 在此基础上， HiMA-R1通过方差减少组相对策略优化（ VR-GRPO ）来控制轨迹的上下文预算，该方法沿着初始轨迹进行采样，以便采样成本随轨迹长度增加而增加，并更新具有最大奖励方差的代理，以及轨迹长度的效率奖励和内存进化机制，该机制将GRPO奖励重新用作保持在上下文中的免费信号。

Agents built upon 3B and 7B open-source models match DeepSeek-R1 and surpass DeepSeek-V3 by 6.0 points on average, while reward-driven memory reduces the update steps needed for convergence by roughly 55%. 基于3B和7B开源模型构建的代理与DeepSeek-R1匹配，平均超过DeepSeek-V3 6.0分，而奖励驱动的内存将收敛所需的更新步骤减少了大约55%。

A6 From Legacy Fortran to Portable Kokkos: An Autonomous Agentic AI Workflow

arXiv: 2509.12443 OpenAIMetaAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Scientific applications continue to rely on legacy Fortran codebases originally developed for homogeneous, CPU-based systems. 科学应用程序继续依赖于最初为同构、基于CPU的系统开发的传统FORTRAN代码库。

As High-Performance Computing (HPC) shifts toward heterogeneous GPU-accelerated architectures, many accelerators lack native Fortran bindings, creating an urgent need to modernize legacy codes for portability. 随着高性能计算（ HPC ）转向异构GPU加速架构，许多加速器缺乏本机FORTRAN绑定，迫切需要对遗留代码进行现代化改造以实现可移植性。

These trajectories are optimized jointly with on-policy rollouts under a mixed-behavior objective that mitigates their distribution mismatch via importance sampling.

Experiments across reasoning diverse datasets and agentic benchmarks demonstrate that PICO consistently outperforms standard RLVR and prior off-policy baselines.

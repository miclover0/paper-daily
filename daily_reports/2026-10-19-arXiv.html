A1 Can LLMs predict the outcomes of machine learning experiments?

arXiv: 2610.22599 AnthropicKimiRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Recent advances in AI make Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) increasingly plausible. 人工智能的最新进展使递归自我改进（ RSI ）变得越来越合理。

Large Language Models (LLMs) can already propose, select, and run Machine Learning (ML) experiments. 大型语言模型（ LLM ）已经可以提出、选择和运行机器学习（ ML ）实验。

However, the space of possible design choices in ML is vast. 然而，机器学习中可能的设计选择空间是巨大的。

An LLM that proposes an experiment needs a reliable intuition of whether it is worth running given the timing constraints of long training runs. 提出实验的LLM需要一个可靠的直觉，即考虑到长时间训练运行的时间限制，它是否值得运行。

In this work, we measure such intuition in current frontier LLMs by evaluating how accurately they predict the impact of self-generated ML experiment ideas before these are put into training runs. 在这项工作中，我们通过评估当前前沿LLM在投入训练运行之前预测自生ML实验想法影响的准确程度来衡量这种直觉。

To this end, we construct a dataset of 288 LLM-proposed ideas on eight MLE-bench tasks. 为此，我们构建了一个包含288个LLM提出的关于八个MLE-bench任务的想法的数据集。

We then prompt five frontier LLMs (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5.1, Kimi K3, and GLM-5.3) to predict the impact of each idea and compare their predictions with the actual outcomes after training. 然后，我们提示五位前沿LLM （ GPT-6 Astra、Claude Opus 5.5、Claude Fable 5.1、Kimi K3和GLM-5.3 ）预测每个想法的影响，并将他们的预测与培训后的实际结果进行比较。

We report four main findings. 我们报告了四个主要发现。

First, LLM predictions are substantially better than random chance but far from accurate: even the best model, Claude Opus 5.5, explains only half of the variance in outcomes (R^2 = 0.49). 首先， LLM预测大大优于随机机会，但远非准确：即使是最好的模型， Claude Opus 5.5 ，也只能解释结果方差的一半（ R ^ 2 = 0.49 ）。

Second, we notice that all tested models systematically underestimate the magnitude of the changes. 其次，我们注意到所有测试模型都系统地低估了变化的幅度。

Third, prediction quality varies widely across tasks. 第三，不同任务的预测质量差异很大。

Finally, the choice of the coding harness that actually implements the ideas has little effect on outcomes, and we find no evidence of self-preference. 最后，实际实现这些想法的编码线束的选择对结果几乎没有影响，我们没有发现自我偏好的证据。

Higher reasoning settings and additional context lead to modest gains. 更高的推理设置和额外的背景会导致适度的收益。

Overall, our results show that current LLMs have useful but limited intuition about which ML ideas will work. 总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，当前的LLM对哪些ML创意有效有着有用但有限的直觉。

We release our dataset to support further study. 我们发布数据集以支持进一步的研究。

A2 AlphaBet: Reinforcement Learning for LLM Event Forecasting Agents

arXiv: 2610.22684 Agentic RLAgentLLM

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Forecasting an unresolved event requires finding relevant evidence and deciding how strongly it supports each outcome. 预测未解决的事件需要找到相关证据，并决定其对每个结果的支持程度。

Yet outcome-based training of Large Language Model (LLM) forecasters often begins with preassembled evidence at a single prediction date, leaving evidence acquisition outside the learning loop. 然而，大型语言模型（ LLM ）预测人员的基于结果的培训通常始于单个预测日期的预组装证据，而将证据获取置于学习循环之外。

We introduce AlphaBet, an agentic reinforcement learning (RL) framework that trains a single LLM policy to search, reason, and report probabilities across multiple pre-resolution dates. 我们引入了AlphaBet ，这是一个代理强化学习(RL)框架，用于训练单个LLM策略，以便在多个预解析日期中搜索、推理和报告概率。

Each dated forecast is scored by a proper scoring rule against the eventual outcome, allowing one resolved event to supervise decisions under different information states. 每个过时的预测都根据适当的评分规则对最终结果进行评分，从而允许一个已解决的事件监督不同信息状态下的决策。

We decompose forecasting regret into an evidence-acquisition gap and a probability-reporting gap, and show that Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)'s standard-deviation normalization can distort the reporting target under multi-class proper scores. 我们将预测遗憾分解为证据获取缺口和概率报告缺口，并表明组相对策略优化（ GRPO ）的标准偏差归一化可以在多类正确分数下扭曲报告目标。

On GLM-4.5-Air, AlphaBet reduces the Brier score from 0.731 to 0.626 on Forecast-Dojo and from 0.612 to 0.528 on FutureX. 在GLM-4.5-Air上， AlphaBet将Forecast-Dojo上的Brier评分从0.731降至0.626 ，在FutureX上从0.612降至0.528。

Controlled ablations attribute these gains to multi-date training and show that they come from improvements in both information acquisition and probability reporting. 受控消融将这些收益归因于多日期培训，并表明它们来自信息获取和概率报告的改进。

Our empirical results validate that GRPO's std normalization degrades proper-scoring-rule performance while leaving top-outcome accuracy largely unchanged. 我们的实证结果验证了GRPO的STD归一化会降低正确的评分规则性能，同时保持最高结果的准确性基本不变。

A3 Benchmarking and Enhancing Agentic Causal Discovery

arXiv: 2610.22846 AnthropicQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

LLM agents can now run causal discovery algorithms on a dataset and report what comes out. LLM代理现在可以在数据集上运行因果发现算法并报告结果。

Whether they do more than that, such as noticing where an algorithm went wrong and fixing it with further tests, is hard to tell from current benchmarks. 他们是否做得更多，例如注意到算法出错的地方并通过进一步的测试来修复它，很难从当前的基准中分辨出来。

Most of them give the variables meaningful names, and an LLM shown only the names of the T\"ubingen cause-effect pairs already gets 99% of the directions right. 他们中的大多数给了变量有意义的名称，并且仅显示T\ "ubingen因果对的名称的LLM已经获得了99 ％的方向正确。

With the names removed, the data are usually clean enough that picking a better algorithm than PC closes most of the gap. 删除名称后，数据通常足够干净，以至于选择比PC更好的算法可以缩小大部分差距。

We introduce Heuresis, a benchmark of anonymous simulated datasets whose mechanisms range from linear Gaussian to heavy-tailed. 我们引入Heuresis ，这是匿名模拟数据集的基准，其机制范围从线性高斯到重尾。

For each family of datasets it separates what a better existing algorithm can gain from what only a closer look at the data can gain, and it traces every edge an agent changes. 对于每个数据集系列，它将更好的现有算法从只有仔细查看数据才能获得的收益分开，并跟踪代理更改的每个边缘。

Every family leaves a gap that none of the algorithms, the router and the two causal-discovery foundation models we ran can close, so a score above the best chosen algorithm has to come from using the data. 每个家庭都留下了一个我们运行的算法、路由器和两个因果发现基础模型都无法填补的空白，因此使用数据必须获得高于最佳选择的算法的分数。

We instantiate the benchmark with an agent harness and three LLMs, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 and Qwen 3.8 Flash. 我们使用代理线束和三个LLM （ GPT-6.1 SOL、Claude Opus 5.5和Qwen 3.8 Flash ）实例化基准。

GPT-6.1 Sol surpasses the best chosen algorithm on all six families, by 14% adjacency F1 on the most difficult one, and Claude Opus 5.5 does so by 7.4% there. GPT-6.1 SOL在所有六个系列中都超过了最佳选择算法，在最困难的系列中相邻F1的比例为14% ， Claude Opus 5.5在这方面的比例为7.4%。

A third of the true edges the two agents restore on the two hardest families appear in no algorithm's output. 两个智能体在两个最难的族上恢复的真实边缘的三分之一出现在没有算法的输出中。

The code and data will be publicly released to facilitate future work. 代码和数据将公开发布，以方便未来的工作。

A4 Tree-Aware Posterior Distillation for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning

arXiv: 2610.23181 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards has become a scalable post-training paradigm for large language models. 具有可验证奖励的强化学习已成为大型语言模型的可扩展训练后范例。

However, training agents over long-horizon interactions presents two challenges: using only terminal rewards makes turn-level credit assignment sparse and high-variance, while sequential mini-batch updates on a fixed rollout batch introduce policy staleness as the current policy drifts from the behavior policy. 然而，通过长距离交互培训代理存在两个挑战：仅使用终端奖励会使回合级信用分配稀疏且高方差，而固定推出批次的连续小批量更新会随着当前策略偏离行为策略而导致策略失效。

Existing approaches obtain denser credit via learned critics, process reward models, or on-policy distillation, but they rely on a value network, step-level annotations, or a stronger external teacher that increase training complexity. 现有的方法通过学习批评、流程奖励模型或策略提炼获得更密集的信誉，但它们依赖于价值网络、阶梯级注释或更强大的外部教师，从而增加了培训复杂性。

Meanwhile, importance sampling and clipping mitigate policy drift, but they do not specify a finite target that stops sequential updates from overshooting. 与此同时，重要性采样和裁剪可以缓解策略漂移，但它们没有指定阻止连续更新超调的有限目标。

In this work, we introduce TreeOPSD, a critic-free, tree-aware posterior self-distillation framework for long-horizon agentic reinforcement learning. 在这项工作中，我们引入了TreeOPSD ，这是一种无批评、树感知的后验自馏框架，用于长视野代理强化学习。

Treating each interaction turn as a tree node, TreeOPSD branches alternative continuations from shared prefixes and recursively backs up terminal rewards into implicit turn-level credit. 将每个交互转换视为树节点， TreeOPSD从共享前缀分支替代延续，并递归地将终端奖励备份为隐式转换级信用。

It then maps each credit to a closed-form, bounded teacher distribution parameterized by the likelihood ratio relative to the frozen behavior policy. 然后，它将每个学分映射到一个封闭形式的、有界的教师分布，该分布由相对于冻结行为策略的似然比参数化。

Minimizing the divergence between this teacher and the student policy recovers the policy-gradient direction as the tangent at the behavior policy while bounding updates toward a finite target across sequential mini-batches. 最小化该教师和学生策略之间的差异可以恢复策略梯度方向，作为行为策略的切线，同时跨越连续的小批次将更新限制在有限目标上。

Empirically, TreeOPSD improves agentic capabilities across several competitive benchmarks and model scales, especially for long-horizon tasks, e.g., raising the average accuracy of Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking-2507 by 4.97 points over GRPO on the Deep Research setting. 根据经验， TreeOPSD在多个竞争基准和模型尺度上提高了代理能力，特别是对于长期任务，例如，在Deep Research设置上， Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking-2507的平均准确度比GRPO提高4.97分。

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Workflow progression and completion require machine-checkable repository, deployment, and runtime evidence, with recovery constrained by explicit operational bounds and termination criteria. 工作流的进展和完成需要机器可检查的存储库、部署和运行时证据，恢复受明确的操作界限和终止标准的限制。

We instantiate the framework on Google Cloud and evaluate repository completeness, controlled execution, evidence-gated progression, operational deployment, and bounded recovery. 我们在Google Cloud上实例化该框架，并评估存储库的完整性、受控执行、证据门限进度、运营部署和有限恢复。

Experimental results show that executions terminate with either a verified operational cloud deployment or an auditable terminal failure under bounded recovery. 实验结果表明，在有限恢复下，执行终止于经过验证的运营云部署或可审计的终端故障。

The framework provides a unified engineering architecture for cloud-based workflows spanning Agentic DevOps, Agentic CloudOps, Agentic SRE/AIOps, Agentic SecOps, Agentic DataOps, Agentic MLOps/LLMOps, AgentOps, Agentic RAG/GraphRAG, and related cloud-engineering domains. 该框架为跨Agentic DevOps、Agentic CloudOps、Agentic SRE/AIOps、Agentic SecOps、Agentic DataOps、Agentic MLOps/LLMOps、AgentOps、Agentic RAG/GraphRAG和相关云工程域的基于云的工作流提供了统一的工程架构。

A1 SCOPE: Selective Critical-Step On-Policy Self-Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

arXiv: 2610.38391 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Outcome-based reinforcement learning provides coarse supervision for long-horizon LLM agents: failed trajectories may contain correct steps, while successful trajectories can contain errors. 基于结果的强化学习为长期LLM代理提供粗略的监督：失败的轨迹可能包含正确的步骤，而成功的轨迹可能包含错误。

Trajectory-level skills can also shift teacher preferences at unrelated steps, introducing off-target distillation signals. 轨迹级技能还可以在不相关的步骤中改变教师的偏好，从而引入脱靶蒸馏信号。

We introduce SCOPE, a framework for Selective Critical-Step On-Policy Self-Distillation. 我们引入了SCOPE ，这是一个选择性关键步骤随政策自蒸馏的框架。

After a supervised warm-up, the policy analyzes its own trajectories, generates step-specific hindsight skills, and distills their guidance only at selected error steps. 在监督热身后，该政策会分析其自身的轨迹，生成特定步骤的后见之明技能，并仅在选定的错误步骤中提取其指导。

A bounded signed teacher-student coefficient stabilizes auxiliary updates while preserving positive reinforcement and negative correction. 有界有符号师生系数稳定辅助更新，同时保持正强化和负校正。

A success-relative pairwise length reward further encourages efficient task completion. 相对于成功的成对长度奖励进一步鼓励高效完成任务。

Experiments on ALFWorld and WebShop demonstrate consistent improvements across multiple model backbones. 在ALFWorld和WebShop上进行的实验表明，在多个模型主干上进行了一致的改进。

With Qwen2.5-3B, SCOPE achieves 97.7% success on ALFWorld and 85.2% on WebShop, outperforming SEED by 5.9 and 6.3 percentage points, respectively. 借助Qwen2.5-3B ， SCOPE在ALFWorld上取得了97.7%的成功，在WebShop上取得了85.2%的成功，分别比SEED高出5.9和6.3个百分点。

On unseen ALFWorld tasks, SCOPE reaches 89.6% success, improving over GRPO by 23.2 percentage points with 46.7% fewer interactions. 在不可见的ALFWorld任务上，范围成功率达到89.6% ，比GRPO提高了23.2个百分点，互动减少了46.7%。

Scaling to Qwen3-32B, SCOPE achieves 99.2% success on seen ALFWorld tasks and 94.0% on unseen tasks. 扩展到Qwen3-32B ， SCOPE在可见的ALFWorld任务上取得了99.2 ％的成功，在看不见的任务上取得了94.0 ％的成功。

A2 TreasuryQA Bench: Can AI Agents Work an Archive of Scanned Financial Records?

arXiv: 2610.38924 OpenAIAnthropicAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Organisations keep decades of financial records as scanned PDFs on shared drives, and AI agents can now reach those drives through Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. 组织在共享驱动器上扫描PDF时会保留数十年的财务记录， AI代理现在可以通过模型上下文协议(MCP)服务器访问这些驱动器。

Existing benchmarks do not mirror this production setting because they simplify the task. 现有基准不会镜像此生产设置，因为它们简化了任务。

In some, the relevant documents are handed to the agent, so it never has to search. 在某些情况下，相关文件会交给代理，因此无需进行搜索。

In others, the agent searches through documents with pre-extracted text, so it never has to read scanned images. 在其他情况下，客服代表使用预先提取的文本搜索文件，因此无需阅读扫描的图像。

We introduce TreasuryQA Bench, which requires both: 70 questions over 1,435 scanned U.S. 我们引入了TreasuryQA Bench ，它要求两者： 70个问题，超过1,435个扫描的美国

Treasury publications, held in Veris's high-fidelity Dropbox twin. 财务出版物，由Veris的高保真Dropbox双胞胎持有。

In each task the agent searches for the relevant file through Dropbox's MCP server, reads the pages it needs with Reducto's hosted OCR server or its own tools, and replies with one exact figure. 在每项任务中，代理通过Dropbox的MCP服务器搜索相关文件，使用Reducto的托管OCR服务器或自己的工具读取所需页面，并回复一个确切的数字。

A Python grader checks each answer against an accepted range around the correct value. Python评分员对照正确值周围的接受范围检查每个答案。

In this paper we present results from ten agents. 在本文中，我们展示了来自10个代理的结果。

The best, Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code, solves 64.8% of attempts and the worst, GPT-5.6 Terra in Codex, solves 16.7%, so the benchmark is far from saturated. 最好的是Claude Code中的Claude Opus 5.5 ，解决了64.8%的尝试，最坏的是Codex中的GPT-5.6 TERRA ，解决了16.7% ，因此基准远未饱和。

We also test how scan quality affects performance: every agent solves fewer tasks on the blurriest scans. 我们还会测试扫描质量如何影响性能：每位客服代表在最模糊的扫描中解决的任务较少。

Most failed attempts reply with a wrong number rather than no number, and the most common cause is a digit misread from the scan. 大多数失败的尝试使用错误的号码而不是没有号码进行回复，最常见的原因是扫描中的数字误读。

Three worked examples are included with their answer keys. 他们的答案键包括三个工作示例。

A3 Pre-Crime for Agents, at the Last Second: Probing When a Recruited Agent Gives In

arXiv: 2610.40588 QwenAgentMulti-Agent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

When AI agents share channels and credentials, one agent can press another to break its operator's rule, a first step towards collusion. 当人工智能代理共享频道和凭据时，一个代理可以按另一个来打破其操作员的规则，这是勾结的第一步。

Output filters check only what an agent has already written. 输出过滤器仅检查客服代表已编写的内容。

Can an overseer tell beforehand that an agent is about to give in? 监督员能事先告知代理商即将屈服吗？

We show that a linear probe on the agent's activations can, in a controlled multi-agent setting. 我们表明，在受控的多药剂设置中，可以对药剂的激活进行线性探测。

Across six scenarios, a peer presses a target for up to 20 rounds to reveal a secret, such as a deploy token; the target often refuses, then gives in. 在六个场景中，同行按下目标最多20轮以揭示秘密，例如部署令牌；目标通常拒绝，然后屈服。

Read at each model call before its output is written, the probe picks out the one that leaks. 在写入输出之前，在每个模型调用中读取，探针会挑选出泄漏的模型调用。

On held-out Qwen3-32B episodes, at a threshold set to flag 5% of benign calls, it catches 82% of leaking calls. 在保留的Qwen3-32B剧集中，在设置为标记5%的良性呼叫的阈值时，它捕获了82%的泄漏呼叫。

At a similar flag rate, a zero-shot decision-model monitor catches 26%. 在类似的标记率下，零拍决策模型监视器捕获26%。

On gpt-oss-20b, a probe on its reasoning reaches AUROC 0.91. 在gpt-oss-20b上，其推理探针达到AUROC 0.91。

A bag-of-words classifier trained on the same calls comes close, so most of the gain comes from training on labels, not activations. 在相同呼叫上训练的词袋分类器非常接近，因此大部分收益来自标签训练，而不是激活。

The probe also flags permitted disclosures: it reads the coming disclosure more than the violation. 调查还标记了允许的披露：它更多地解读了即将到来的披露，而不是违规行为。

The warning is short-range, and halting on any alarm would stop most benign episodes, which a second text check does not fix. 警告是短距离的，停止任何警报都会阻止大多数良性发作，第二次文本检查无法解决此问题。

A warning raised before generation could let an overseer stop an agent that is about to give in, ahead of others acting on its messages or recruitment spreading through a swarm. 在一代人之前发出的警告可能会让监督员阻止即将屈服的特工，先于其他人根据其信息采取行动或通过群体进行招募。

A4 Vendster: An Evaluation Arena to Investigate How Forecasting Tool Quality Affects LLM Agent Behaviors in Buyer-Vendor Negotiation

arXiv: 2610.38292 OpenAIAnthropicDeepSeek

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Time-series forecasters are ranked almost entirely by accuracy metrics, but a forecast's decision value, its effect on the downstream decision, need not track accuracy and is rarely measured. 时间序列预测者几乎完全按照准确性指标进行排名，但预测的决策值及其对下游决策的影响不需要跟踪准确性，也很少被测量。

This problem compounds as large language model (LLM) agents increasingly consume forecasters as tools, mediating them through stochastic, opaque policies. 这个问题变得更加复杂，因为大型语言模型（ LLM ）代理越来越多地将预测者用作工具，通过随机、不透明的政策来调解他们。

We present Vendster, an evaluation arena measuring how a forecaster's bias and prediction-interval width change the outcome of a buyer--vendor discount negotiation between two LLM agents. 我们介绍Vendster ，这是一个评估领域，用于衡量预测者的偏见和预测间隔宽度如何改变两个LLM代理之间买方-卖方折扣谈判的结果。

In the reference instance Vendster-M5, sampling base demand patterns from the M5 dataset, we evaluate two frontier-closed LLM families (Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.6 Sol) and two open ones (DeepSeek-V3.2 and Qwen3-235B-A22B). 在参考实例Vendster-M5中，从M5数据集中抽样基本需求模式，我们评估了两个前沿封闭LLM系列（ Claude Opus 4.6和GPT-5.6 Sol ）和两个开放LLM系列（ DeepSeek-V3.2和Qwen3-235B-A22B ）。

Model-generated forecasts raise sales target attainment by up to +17 percentage points (p<0.001), yet standard forecast evaluation metrics such as relWQL and MASE do not predict negotiation outcomes at a statistically significant level. 模型生成的预测将销售目标实现率提高了17个百分点（ p < 0.001 ） ，但relWQL和MASE等标准预测评估指标并未预测具有统计学意义的谈判结果。

To isolate the causal structure, we substitute in synthetic forecasts with controlled bias and interval width. 为了隔离因果结构，我们在综合预测中用受控偏差和区间宽度代替。

Under-prediction bias significantly improves outcomes for agents that react more to the forecasts---dose-response slopes -0.387 and -0.416 (p<0.001). 低预测偏倚显著改善对预测反应较多的药物的结果--剂量反应斜率-0.387和-0.416 （ p < 0.001 ）。

Interval width has no statistically significant effect at zero bias. 在零偏差下，间隔宽度没有统计学显著影响。

These findings indicate that the direction of forecast bias and agent forecast-reactivity are more strongly associated with downstream outcomes than forecast accuracy. 这些发现表明，预测偏差和客服代表预测反应性的方向与下游结果的关联性比预测准确性更强。

arXiv: 2509.12443 OpenAIQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Scientific applications still rely heavily on legacy Fortran codebases built for homogeneous, CPU-based systems. 科学应用程序仍然严重依赖为同构、基于CPU的系统构建的传统FORTRAN代码库。

As High-Performance Computing (HPC) shifts toward GPU-accelerated architectures, many accelerators lack native Fortran bindings, creating a need to translate and optimize legacy code for portability. 随着高性能计算（ HPC ）向GPU加速架构的转变，许多加速器缺乏本机FORTRAN绑定，因此需要翻译和优化遗留代码以实现可移植性。

Kokkos offers performance portability through a single-source C++ abstraction, but manual porting from Fortran demands significant expertise and time. Kokkos通过单源C + +抽象提供性能可移植性，但从Fortran手动移植需要大量的专业知识和时间。

While large language models (LLMs) show promise in source-to-source code generation, their use in autonomous workflows for translating and optimizing parallel code is largely unexplored. 虽然大型语言模型（ LLM ）在源代码到源代码生成方面显示出希望，但它们在自主工作流程中用于翻译和优化并行代码的用途在很大程度上尚未被探索。

We evaluate the pipeline over five runs per configuration on an NVIDIA A100, characterizing its variability in success rates, token cost, and optimization behavior, on benchmark kernels including a real-world multi-file kernel from a production climate model. 我们评估了NVIDIA A100上每个配置五次以上的流水线，表征了其在成功率、代币成本和优化行为方面的可变性，基准内核包括来自生产气候模型的真实世界多文件内核。

A6 Compact-Bench: Measuring What Context Compaction Costs Long-Horizon Coding Agents

arXiv: 2610.31710 AnthropicDeepSeekKimi

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Context compaction lets long-horizon coding agents continue beyond their context windows, but can erase the state needed to finish a task. 上下文压缩允许长距离编码代理继续超出其上下文窗口，但可以擦除完成任务所需的状态。

Prior evaluations measure efficiency, retained information, or end-to-end success, leaving the effect of a specific history replacement difficult to isolate. 先前的评估衡量效率、保留的信息或端到端的成功，使特定历史记录替换的影响难以隔离。

Compact-Bench pairs full-history and summary-only continuations from a shared repository state, and evaluates recurring details and successive production compactions. Compact-Bench将共享存储库状态的完整历史记录和仅总结的延续配对，并评估重复的详细信息和连续的生产压缩。

It covers 35 SWE-bench repairs, 146 agentic sessions, and 106 production chains. 它涵盖35个SWE-bench维修， 146个代理会话和106个生产链。

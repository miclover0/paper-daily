A1 AttnSR: Attention Step Reward for Long-Horizon Agentic RL

arXiv: 2611.07932 QwenAgentic RLAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Long-horizon LLM agents make multi-step decisions, yet outcome-reward-only reinforcement learning provides little direct supervision for individual steps. 长远的LLM代理会做出多步决策，但只有结果奖励的强化学习很少对单个步骤进行直接监督。

We introduce AttnSR, a step-level reward augmentation method that derives reward allocations from policy attention extracted during training replay, without a separately trained reward model or additional rollouts. 我们引入了AttnSR ，这是一种阶梯级奖励增强方法，它从训练重播期间提取的策略注意力中获取奖励分配，而无需单独训练的奖励模型或额外的推出。

AttnSR traces backward through the policy model attention graph to estimate the relevance of earlier steps to the final response. AttnSR通过政策模型注意力图向后跟踪，以估计早期步骤与最终响应的相关性。

The terminal step, measured relative to a running baseline, determines whether these rewards reinforce or penalize the selected steps. 相对于运行基线测量的最终步骤确定这些奖励是加强还是惩罚所选步骤。

In a controlled Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct study, AttnSR consistently outperforms the PPO baseline across all evaluated checkpoints on ALFWorld, raising final held-out success from 91.2% to 97.1%. 在一项对照的Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct研究中， AttnSR在ALFWorld上所有评估检查点的表现始终优于PPO基线，将最终成功率从91.2%提高到97.1%。

The ablation experiment shows that the benefit depends on how intermediate rewards are assigned, rather than simple accumulation of step rewards over time. 消融实验表明，好处取决于如何分配中间奖励，而不是随着时间的推移简单地积累步骤奖励。

AttnSR also improves ScienceWorld at every checkpoint while cutting environment interactions by 29.9%. AttnSR还在每个检查点改进了ScienceWorld ，同时将环境交互减少了29.9%。

These results support policy attention as a practical prior for step-level reward allocation in long-horizon agentic RL. 这些结果支持政策关注作为长期代理RL中阶梯级奖励分配的实际先例。

Code is available at https://github.com/fairyshine/AttnSR . 优惠码可在https://github.com/fairyshine/AttnSR上获得。

A2 Who Should Know What? Empirical Information Design for Multi-Agent LLM Economies

arXiv: 2611.07667 AnthropicGoogleAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

The performance of multi-agent LLM systems depends not only on the capabilities of the underlying models but also on information-sharing policies, which govern what information each agent observes and at what level of detail. 多代理LLM系统的性能不仅取决于底层模型的能力，还取决于信息共享策略，这些策略控制着每个代理观察的信息以及细节级别。

But does providing AI agents with more detailed information always improve collective outcomes, or can transparency paradoxically degrade efficiency? 但是，向人工智能代理提供更详细的信息是否总能改善集体结果，还是透明度会自相矛盾地降低效率？

To investigate this, we introduce empirical information design, an experimental framework that systematically compares disclosure policies across economic markets populated by LLM agents, while holding interaction rules and models fixed. 为了调查这一点，我们引入了实证信息设计，这是一个实验框架，系统地比较了LLM代理人所在经济市场的披露政策，同时保持了交互规则和模型的固定。

We evaluate ten frontier LLMs in multi-resource trade, bilateral bargaining, and double auctions, using ground-truth utilities to decouple identified opportunities from realized outcomes. 我们评估了多资源贸易、双边谈判和双重拍卖中的十个前沿LLM ，使用地面实用工具将已识别的机会与已实现的结果分离。

We find that finer information disclosure yields highly model-dependent results. 我们发现，更精细的信息披露会产生高度依赖模型的结果。

In trade, revealing exact valuations rather than ordinal preference rankings reduces realized gains for GPT-6 Sol (from 98% to 91% of maximum) yet increases them for Claude Sonnet 5 (from 72% to 86%). 在贸易中，透露确切估值而不是序数偏好排名会降低GPT-6 SOL的实际收益（从最大值的98%增加到91% ） ，但会增加Claude Sonnet 5的实际收益（从72%增加到86% ）。

In bargaining, exact information leads Grok 4.7 to produce less balanced agreements, whereas Claude Opus 5.5 divides gains symmetrically. 在讨价还价中，确切的信息导致Grok 4.7产生不太平衡的协议，而Claude Opus 5.5则对称地划分收益。

In double auctions, full disclosure inflates price demands, triggering market breakdown and lowering welfare for Gemini 3.1 Pro (97% to 78%), Claude Opus 5.5 (94% to 64%), and Grok 4.7 (97% to 62%). 在双重拍卖中，全面披露会夸大价格需求，引发市场崩溃，并降低Gemini 3.1 Pro （ 97%至78% ）、Claude Opus 5.5 （ 94%至64% ）和Grok 4.7 （ 97%至62% ）的福利。

Overall, these results suggest that model selection and information design must be co-optimized, as LLMs vary in how their strategic responses to information impact system objectives. 总体而言，这些结果表明，模型选择和信息设计必须共同优化，因为LLM对信息系统目标的战略响应各不相同。

A3 Signed but Not Safe: Requirement Integrity in Multi-Agent LLM Handoffs

arXiv: 2611.07483 OpenAIQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Enterprise AI agents increasingly split a user's request across subagents and merge their results, and each handoff can summarize away, alter or ignore a requirement. 企业人工智能代理越来越多地将用户的请求拆分到子代理并合并其结果，每个切换都可以总结、更改或忽略某个要求。

We report a preregistered study with synthetic, exactly scorable tasks comparing free-text, structured JSON and signed requirement-manifest handoffs: the orchestrator extracts each requirement as a verbatim quote plus a checkable predicate, code signs the manifest with HMAC-SHA256, and deterministic code verifies the merger's decision. 我们报告了一项预先注册的研究，其中包含合成的、完全可评分的任务，比较了自由文本、结构化JSON和签名的需求清单切换：编排器将每个需求提取为逐字报价加上可检查的谓词，使用HMAC-SHA256对清单进行代码签名，并确定性代码验证合并的决定。

With Qwen3-8B (1,440 trajectories, five threats), signed manifests cut unsafe commits under attack from 69.4% to 9.0% (60.4 pp, 95% CI [51.4, 69.4]) with no net loss of conformant commits relative to structured handoffs. 通过Qwen3-8B （ 1,440个轨迹， 5个威胁） ，签名清单将受到攻击的不安全提交从69.4%减少到9.0% （ 60.4 pp ， 95% CI [51.4 ， 69.4] ） ，并且没有与结构化切换相关的符合提交净损失。

Signing is what defeats in-transit tampering: an unsigned manifest committed violations in 79% of mutated runs, the signed one in none. 签名是击败在途篡改的原因：未签名的清单在79%的变异运行中犯下了违规行为，而签名的清单则没有。

On identical trajectories the deterministic verifier caught 78.7% of violating decisions versus 59.0% for an LLM checker, with 7% versus 50% false alarms. 在相同的轨迹上，确定性验证者发现了78.7%的违规决策，而LLM检查者为59.0% ，错误警报率为7%对50%。

An oracle manifest removed all remaining unsafe commits: the residual risk comes from extraction errors made before signing. Oracle清单删除了所有剩余的不安全提交：剩余风险来自签名前发生的提取错误。

A preregistered pre-signing injection attack confirmed this boundary: one adversarial line raised signed-manifest violations from 14.6% to 37.5% (Qwen3-8B), 11.1% to 63.9% (gpt-oss-20b) and 8.3% to 75.0% (GPT-5.6-luna), and the signature then authenticated the corrupted manifest. 预先注册的预签名注入攻击证实了这一界限：一条对抗线将签名清单违规率从14.6%提高到37.5% （ Qwen3-8B ） ， 11.1%提高到63.9% （ gpt-oss-20b ） ， 8.3%提高到75.0% （ GPT-5.6-luna ） ，然后签名认证了损坏的清单。

Signing protects requirements in transit, not at extraction; the extraction step is the trust boundary. 签名保护传输中的要求，而不是提取中的要求；提取步骤是信任边界。

A4 Octopus: One Brain, Many Branches for Latent-KV Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

arXiv: 2611.06956 QwenAgentMulti-Agent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Multi-agent LLM systems explore in parallel by spawning sub-agents with isolated context windows, whereas single-agent systems append every search and verification step into one growing trajectory. 多代理LLM系统通过生成具有孤立上下文窗口的子代理来并行探索，而单代理系统将每个搜索和验证步骤附加到一个不断增长的轨迹中。

However, existing multi-agent frameworks duplicate prefix KV caches across branches, exchange findings by re-prefilling text summaries, and flatten or drop cross-branch dependencies during reinforcement learning (RL). 然而，现有的多智能体框架跨分支复制前缀KV缓存，通过重新填充文本摘要来交换发现，并在强化学习（ RL ）期间扁平化或放弃跨分支依赖关系。

We introduce Octopus, a single-model multi-agent RL framework in which all threads share one backbone and coordinate over a GPU-resident directed acyclic graph (DAG) of KV states through five primitives (fork, read_thread, wait_threads, rewind, commit). 我们引入了Octopus ，这是一种单模型多代理RL框架，其中所有线程共享一个主干，并通过五个原语（ fork、read_thread、wait_threads、rewind、commit ）在KV状态的GPU驻留定向非循环图（ DAG ）上进行协调。

Octopus combines three co-designed mechanisms: (1) Zero-Copy Radix Forking, which spawns branches as $\mathcal{O}(1)$ cursor views over shared physical KV pages; (2) Attention-Only KV Mounting, which mounts a sibling thread's terminal KV blocks directly into full-attention layers while bypassing linear-recurrent states (Gated DeltaNet / Mamba-2), cutting off-task hijacking by $21\times$ ($57.9\% \to 2.7\%$) while retaining $92.2\%$ of full-text mutual information at zero extra KV allocation; and (3) a Packed DAG-GRPO Trainer, which packs all episode branches into one block-sparse attention pass and assigns counterfactual branch credit via leave-one-branch-out marginal rewards.

Trained end-to-end with GRPO on Qwen3.5-4B over a sparse BM25 corpus, Octopus reaches $49.6\%$ accuracy on BrowseComp-Plus ($+17.5$ points over single-agent GRPO at 143K context and $+12.5$ points over text-summary multi-agent RL) and $51.2\%$ on OpenResearcher, outperforming dense-retrieval agents up to $250\times$ larger while increasing training throughput $3.3\times$ and cutting peak KV memory $2.8\times$. 通过稀疏的BM25语料库在Qwen3.5-4B上使用GRPO进行端到端培训，八达通在BrowseComp-Plus上达到$ 49.6\ % $的准确度（在143K上下文中，单代理GRPO为$ +17.5 $点，文本摘要多代理RL为$ +12.5 $点） ，在OpenResearcher上达到$ 51.2\ % $ ，高于密集检索代理高达$ 250\倍$ ，同时提高培训吞吐量$ 3.3\倍$ ，并削减峰值KV内存$ 2.8\倍$。

A5 The Last Mile to the Real World: Agent-Native Primitives as Reusable Building Blocks for Solving Real-World Tasks

arXiv: 2611.07461 OpenAIAnthropicAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

How can an agent handle a real-world task end to end? 客服代表如何端到端处理真实世界的任务？

Frontier models with their own harnesses still fall short on real-world benchmarks: on Terminal-Bench 4.0, GPT-5.6 Sol with Codex resolves 37.3% of the tasks and Claude Opus 5 with Claude Code 51.8%. 拥有自己线束的前沿型号仍然没有达到现实世界的基准：在Terminal-Bench 4.0上， GPT-5.6 SOL与Codex解决了37.3%的任务，而Claude Opus 5与Claude Code解决了51.8%的任务。

These tasks fail at the step from reaching a resource to applying it, which we call the last mile to the real world. 这些任务在到达资源到应用资源的步骤中失败，我们称之为通往现实世界的最后一英里。

Existing approaches put the resource within reach but leave the competence to apply it with the caller, so each harness rebuilds that competence. 现有的方法使资源触手可及，但将应用资源的能力留给来电者，因此每个线索都会重建这种能力。

We introduce agent-native primitives, reusable units that equip a resource with a resident LLM that applies it in context and communicates with other primitives, and HEELO (Harness Engineering around Evolving Agent-Native Primitives for the Last Mile to Real-WOrld Tasks), a harness engineering framework that builds each task's harness from a library of such primitives. 我们引入了代理原生原语，可重用单元，为资源配备驻留LLM ，将其应用于上下文并与其他原语进行通信，以及HEELO （ Harness Engineering around Evolving Agent-Native Primitives for the Last Mile to Real-WOrld Tasks ） ，这是一个线束工程框架，可从此类原语库构建每个任务的线束。

HEELO composes the primitives a task needs into a task-specific graph, verifies the result against evidence from the environment, revises the primitives a failure implicates, and admits new or improved primitives after validation. HEELO将任务所需的基元组合成特定于任务的图形，根据来自环境的证据验证结果，修改故障涉及的基元，并在验证后承认新的或改进的基元。

We instantiate HEELO with eight primitive families, each built for one kind of task, and evaluate them on more than twenty benchmarks. 我们使用八个原始族来实例化HEELO ，每个原始族都是为一种任务构建的，并在二十多个基准上对其进行评估。

Dev-Primitives raise Terminal-Bench 4.0 resolution from 37.3% under Codex to 55.5% on the same backbone, Harness Primitives composed per task raise Terminal-Bench 2.0 Pass@1 from 60.0% for the seed harness to 72.2%, Tool Primitives raise ToolBench pass rate from 55.8% to 75.1%, and Navigation Primitives raise GAIA-Text-103 accuracy from 45.6% to 58.3%. Dev-Primitives将Terminal-Bench 4.0分辨率从Codex下的37.3%提高到同一骨干网上的55.5% ，每项任务组成的线束图元将种子线束的Terminal-Bench 2.0 Pass @ 1从60.0%提高到72.2% ，工具图元将ToolBench通过率从55.8%提高到75.1% ，导航图元将GAIA-Text-103准确率从45.6%提高到58.3%。

On the 46 real-world tasks of our heelo.dev platform, Claude Opus 5.5 with Claude Code and GPT-6.1 Sol with Codex complete 9 each on their own and 39 and 35 when connected to HEELO through our MCP server, with 1.9$\times$ and 2.3$\times$ less time and 1.55$\times$ lower cost per completed task. 在我们heelo.dev平台的46个真实世界任务中， Claude Code的Claude Opus 5.5和Codex的GPT-6.1 Sol通过我们的MCP服务器连接到HEELO时分别完成了9个和39个和35个任务，每个完成任务的时间减少了1.9 $\ times $ ，时间减少了2.3 $\ times $ ，成本降低了1.55 $\ times $。

A6 ForecastCompass: Guiding Agentic Forecasting with Adaptive Factor Memory

arXiv: 2605.30858 OpenAIGoogleAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Agentic forecasting is important for decision-making in dynamic environments, but it remains challenging because agents must reason from incomplete, time-limited evidence and produce calibrated probabilities before outcomes are resolved. 代理预测对于动态环境中的决策很重要，但它仍然具有挑战性，因为代理必须从不完整、有时间限制的证据中进行推理，并在解决结果之前产生校准的概率。

Memory provides a natural mechanism for transferring experience from resolved forecasts to future prediction tasks. 记忆提供了一种自然机制，用于将经验从已解决的预测转移到未来的预测任务。

Optimal agents tend to acquire power across a wide range of objectives (instrumental convergence).

Two such measures in the context of Markov games are information-theoretic empowerment, the capacity of the channel from an agent's actions to future states, and capability-based individual power, a measure of goal attainment built from the probabilities that a human would attain each goal in a family of candidates were they pursuing it.

We show where the two meet.

From individual power, Fano's inequality yields a floor $B(s)$.

This floor is a lower bound on what we term goal revelation, the mutual information $\mathbb{I}(G;\xi \mid s)$ between the human's latent goal and the realised trajectory.

Empowerment caps revelation from above, so that $B(s) \le \mathbb{I}(G;\xi \mid s) \le \mathbb{I}(\mathbf{A}_h \to \xi \mid s) \le \mathfrak{E}_h(s)$.

A1 The Invisible Gap: Romanized Indic Generation Fails Where Automatic Metrics Succeed

arXiv: 2611.09085 OpenAIAnthropicGoogle

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Hundreds of millions of Indians communicate with digital systems in romanized Latin characters -- typing "aap kaise hain" rather than native script -- yet romanized Indic generation is absent from NLP benchmarks and evaluation frameworks. 数以亿计的印度人以罗马化的拉丁字符与数字系统进行通信-输入“aap kaise hain”而不是本地字母-但NLP基准和评估框架中缺少罗马化的印度语生成。

We demonstrate that automatic metrics (BGE-M3, BERTScore) systematically misrepresent the quality of romanized Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada generation. 我们证明，自动指标（ BGE-M3、BERTScore ）系统地歪曲了罗马化印地语、泰卢固语和卡纳达语生成的质量。

We evaluate four LLMs (Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Gemini 2.5-flash, Qwen3.5-35B) on 142 OpenAssistant query-response pairs. 我们评估了142个OpenAssistant查询-响应对上的四个LLM （ Claude Opus 4.8、GPT-5.5、Gemini 2.5-flash、Qwen3.5-35B ）。

On a 72-response diagnostic subset, native speakers rate GPT-5.5 1.43 points higher than Claude Opus 4.8 on a 12-point rubric, whereas their full-dataset BGE-M3 means differ by only 0.004. 在72反应诊断子集中，母语人士在12点评分标准上比Claude Opus 4.8高出GPT-5.5 1.43分，而其全数据集BGE-M3平均值仅相差0.004。

Two distinct model-specific failure modes emerge: Gemini produces script spillage (16.9% of Telugu responses leak native script), while Qwen collapses to English-only output (16.2% of Kannada responses). 出现了两种不同的模型特定故障模式： Gemini产生脚本溢出（ 16.9 ％的泰卢固语响应泄漏本地脚本） ，而Qwen崩溃为仅英语输出（ 16.2 ％的卡纳达语响应）。

We introduce a failure-aware evaluation policy that assigns zero to empty outputs, script-corrupted responses, and instruction collapses -- revealing failures metrics alone cannot detect. 我们引入了故障感知评估策略，该策略将零分配给空输出、脚本损坏的响应和指令崩溃-仅揭示故障指标无法检测到。

Our results show that evaluating romanized Indic generation requires a coordinated bundle: semantic similarity, script compliance, failure coverage, and native-speaker acceptability. 我们的结果表明，评估罗马化Indic生成需要一个协调的捆绑包：语义相似性、脚本合规性、故障覆盖率和母语者可接受性。

A2 Sentinel Agents for Secure and Trustworthy Agentic AI in Multi-Agent Systems

arXiv: 2509.14956 GoogleQwenAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Multi-agent systems (MAS) built on large language models (LLMs) increasingly share a common conversational space, in which a single adversarial or erroneous message can propagate from one agent to many. 基于大型语言模型（ LLM ）构建的多智能体系统（ MAS ）越来越多地共享一个共同的对话空间，其中单个对抗性或错误消息可以从一个智能体传播到多个智能体。

This paper proposes Sentinel Agents, a dedicated security layer that monitors inter-agent communication with rule-based checks, behavioral analysis and LLM-based semantic inspection, and reports to a Coordinator Agent that enforces policy, for example by quarantining misbehaving agents. 本文提出了Sentinel Agent ，这是一个专用的安全层，通过基于规则的检查、行为分析和基于LLM的语义检查来监控代理之间的通信，并向执行策略的协调代理报告，例如通过隔离行为不端的代理。

We describe the architecture, its deployment patterns and monitoring layers, and we evaluate a continuous-listener prototype on a balanced corpus of 1,000 synthetic messages (500 attacks across five families and 500 benign messages) with two local LLMs. 我们描述了架构、其部署模式和监控层，并使用两个本地LLM在1,000条合成消息（跨五个系列的500次攻击和500条良性消息）的平衡语料库上评估连续监听器原型。

LLM inspection with Gemma 4 12B reached F1 0.86 at a false-positive rate of 0.11, against 0.38 for rules alone. Gemma 4 12B的LLM检查以0.11的假阳性率达到F1 0.86 ，而单独的规则为0.38。

Combining components by logical OR did not improve on it, while a cascade in which only high-precision rules act on their own matched it. 通过逻辑OR组合组件没有改进，而只有高精度规则自行作用的级联与之匹配。

Calibrating the thresholds of the more conservative Qwen3 8B on a development split raised its held-out F1 from 0.56 to 0.80, and on multi-turn conversations the behavioral check detected patterns the rules missed but could not tell legitimate repetition from probing. 在开发分割上校准更保守的Qwen3 8B的阈值将其保留的F1从0.56提高到0.80 ，在多轮会话中，行为检查检测到规则遗漏的模式，但无法区分合法的重复和探测。

The value of combining detectors therefore depends on the model, on its calibration and on the aggregation rule, and the results yield practical guidance for deploying Sentinel Agents. 因此，组合探测器的价值取决于模型、校准和聚合规则，结果为部署Sentinel Agent提供了实用指导。

A3 EvoAgent: An Evolvable Agent Framework with Skill Learning and Multi-Agent Delegation

arXiv: 2604.20133 OpenAIAgentMulti-Agent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

This paper proposes EvoAgent--an evolvable large language model (LLM) agent framework that integrates structured skill learning with a hierarchical sub-agent delegation mechanism. 本文提出了EvoAgent--一种可进化的大型语言模型（ LLM ）代理框架，它将结构化技能学习与分层子代理委派机制相结合。

EvoAgent models skills as multi-file structured capability units equipped with triggering mechanisms and evolutionary metadata, and is designed to enable continuous skill generation and optimization through a user-feedback-driven closed-loop process. EvoAgent将技能建模为配备触发机制和进化元数据的多文件结构化能力单元，旨在通过用户反馈驱动的闭环流程实现持续的技能生成和优化。

In addition, by incorporating a three-stage skill matching strategy and organizing the agent as a harness whose only self-modified component is a persistent, user-owned state (profile, memory and skills), the framework supports dynamic task decomposition for complex problems and long-term capability accumulation. 此外，该框架通过整合三阶段技能匹配策略，将agent组织为一个线束，其唯一的自我修改组件是持久的、用户拥有的状态（配置文件、记忆和技能） ，支持复杂问题的动态任务分解和长期的能力积累。

Experimental results based on real-world foreign trade scenarios demonstrate that, after integrating EvoAgent, GPT-5.2 achieves substantial improvements in professionalism, accuracy, and practical utility. 基于真实世界外贸场景的实验结果表明，在集成EvoAgent后， GPT-5.2在专业性、准确性和实用性方面取得了实质性改进。

Under a five-dimensional LLM-as-Judge evaluation protocol, the overall average score increases by approximately 28%. 在五维LLM-as-Judge评估协议下，总体平均分数提高约28%。

Further model transfer experiments indicate that the performance of an agent system depends not only on the intrinsic capabilities of the underlying model, but also on the degree of synergy between the model and the agent architecture. 进一步的模型转移实验表明，代理系统的性能不仅取决于底层模型的内在能力，还取决于模型与代理架构之间的协同程度。

Code and documentation are released at https://github.com/Focus-AI-Center/Mentarc-EvoAgent; the evaluation corpus contains proprietary business data that cannot be released, so the reported results are for reference only. 代码和文档在https://github.com/Focus-AI-Center/Mentarc-EvoAgent上发布；评估语料库包含无法发布的专有业务数据，因此报告的结果仅供参考。

A4 HarnessMM: Recursive Self-Improvement for Harness Token Efficiency

arXiv: 2610.32813 DeepSeekRSIAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

HarnessMinusMinus (HarnessMM) is a research project on improving the efficiency of agent harnesses, the software coordinating model reasoning, tool execution, and feedback. HarnessMinusMinus （ HarnessMM ）是一个关于提高代理线束效率、软件协调模型推理、工具执行和反馈的研究项目。

This technical report consolidates its shared framework, implementations, and empirical studies across coding, tool-based, and visual-agent settings. 本技术报告整合了其跨编码、基于工具和可视化代理设置的共享框架、实施和实证研究。

Agent harnesses often invoke reasoning after each tool result, even when earlier calls have already determined subsequent actions. 客服代表通常会在每个工具结果后调用推理，即使之前的调用已经确定了后续操作。

HarnessMM addresses this overhead through guarded decision reuse and recursive self-improvement (RSI). HarnessMM通过保护决策重用和递归自我改进（ RSI ）来解决这一开销。

RSI refines author prompts, context settings, and adapter rules using training feedback, with validation selecting deployment configurations. RSI使用培训反馈优化作者提示、上下文设置和适配器规则，并通过验证选择部署配置。

At inference time, a controller reuses resolved decisions while keeping actor weights fixed. 在推理时，控制器重复使用已解决的决策，同时保持参与者权重固定。

Executable Decision Graphs encode actions, arguments, dependencies, and guards. 可执行决策图对操作、参数、依赖关系和保护进行编码。

Guarded Decision Reuse activates eligible decisions from observed feedback, while Certified Callback Substitution executes them through native tool interfaces, replacing redundant reasoning callbacks. Guarded Decision Reuse根据观察到的反馈激活符合条件的决策，而Certified Callback Substitution通过本机工具界面执行这些决策，从而替换冗余的推理回调。

The original actor resumes when new reasoning is required or reuse conditions are unmet. 当需要新的推理或重复使用条件未满足时，原始参与者恢复。

On HumanEval, HarnessMM preserves Codex success at 90.82\% while reducing tokens per task from 66.72k to 47.02k (29.54\% saving). 在HumanEval上， HarnessMM将Codex的成功率保持在90.82\ % ，同时将每个任务的代币从66.72 k减少到47.02 k （节省29.54\ % ）。

For DeepSeek Harness, success improves from 71.43\% to 85.71\% while tokens decrease from 96.28k to 11.62k (87.93\% saving). 对于DeepSeek线束，成功率从71.43\ %提高到85.71\ % ，而代币从96.28 k减少到11.62 k （节省87.93\ % ）。

An MBPP ablation shows 26.91\% lower cost than single-use graphs and 19.52\% lower cost than retained callbacks. MBPP消融的成本比一次性使用图表低26.91\ % ，比保留的回拨成本低19.52\ %。

The report also summarizes the HarnessMM-V visual-agent study and documents optimization overhead and execution time where measured. 该报告还总结了HarnessMM-V视觉代理研究，并在测量时记录了优化开销和执行时间。

Together, these studies investigate guarded decision reuse across diverse agent harness settings. 这些研究共同调查了各种座席线束设置中的谨慎决策重用。

Code is available at https://github.com/YuhuaJiang2002/HarnessMM. 优惠码可在https://github.com/YuhuaJiang2002/HarnessMM上获得。

A5 Internal Safety Collapse in LLMs and Coding Agents

arXiv: 2603.23509 AnthropicAgentLLM

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

LLMs and coding agents can now tackle complex, long-horizon tasks through advances in reinforcement learning, but stronger task-solving capabilities do not necessarily preserve safety alignment. LLM和编码代理现在可以通过强化学习的进步来处理复杂、长远的任务，但更强的任务解决能力并不一定能保持安全一致性。

Recent AI agent incidents involving reward hacking indicate how optimizing for task success can bring learned capabilities into conflict with safety alignment. 最近涉及奖励黑客的AI代理事件表明，优化任务成功如何使学习到的能力与安全一致性发生冲突。

In this work, we identify a critical failure mode that manifests this conflict, which we term Internal Safety Collapse (ISC). 在这项工作中，我们确定了体现这种冲突的关键故障模式，我们称之为内部安全崩溃（ ISC ）。

When completing otherwise benign coding tasks, AI agents can prioritize satisfying program requirements over safety constraints, generating large volumes of sensitive content, including unsafe outputs, as task data. 在完成其他良性编码任务时，人工智能代理可以优先满足程序要求，而不是安全约束，从而生成大量敏感内容，包括不安全的输出，作为任务数据。

To study this phenomenon, we introduce TVD (Task, Validator, Data), a framework for building controlled workflows that require sensitive content for completion. 为了研究这种现象，我们引入了TVD （ Task ， Validator ， Data ）框架，用于构建需要敏感内容才能完成的受控工作流程。

Through extensive experiments, we observe instances of highly harmful content that closely resemble outputs from early, unaligned LLMs in 2023. 通过广泛的实验，我们观察到了高度有害的内容，这些内容与2023年早期未对齐的LLM的输出非常相似。

We further find that ISC can be effectively triggered even in highly capable LLMs such as Claude Fable 5. 我们进一步发现，即使在Claude Fable 5等能力强大的LLM中，也可以有效地触发ISC。

These results indicate that, despite substantial alignment efforts, frontier LLMs remain internally unsafe: safety alignment primarily reshapes surface behavior without eliminating the underlying knowledge that enables unsafe outputs. 这些结果表明，尽管做出了大量调整努力，但前沿LLM在内部仍然不安全：安全调整主要是重塑表面行为，而不会消除导致不安全输出的潜在知识。

Our findings underscore the need for caution when deploying LLMs in high-stakes settings, such as scientific pipelines and autonomous agents. 我们的研究结果强调了在高风险环境中部署LLM时需要谨慎，例如科学管道和自主代理。

A6 Learning to Persuade Exposes How Easily LLMs Abandon Correct Beliefs

arXiv: 2608.11624 OpenAIMetaQwen

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Persuasion is a core dynamic of natural language communication, shaping how large language models (LLMs) update beliefs, resolve disagreements, and reach decisions. 说服力是自然语言交流的核心动力，塑造了大型语言模型（ LLM ）如何更新信念、解决分歧和做出决策。

As LLMs increasingly debate, advise, and think collaboratively with humans and each other, resistance to harmful persuasion becomes a core requirement for reliable behavior. 随着法学硕士越来越多地与人类和彼此合作进行辩论、建议和思考，抵制有害的说服成为可靠行为的核心要求。

Yet a single targeted persuasive argument can be enough to collapse model accuracy to near zero, even when the argument is factually false. 然而，一个有针对性的有说服力的论点就足以将模型的准确性压缩到接近零，即使该论点实际上是错误的。

We formalize this threat as adversarial persuasion and introduce an adversarial reinforcement learning framework that trains persuader agents to change a target model's answer in a single interaction. 我们将这种威胁形式化为对抗性说服，并引入对抗性强化学习框架，训练说服代理在单次交互中改变目标模型的答案。

First, we show that RL-trained persuaders raise persuasion success from approximately 24% to over 93% against the training-time persuadee. 首先，我们表明，受过强化学习训练的说服者将说服成功率从大约24%提高到93%以上。

A1 BlenderWorld: Benchmarking Visual Agents for 3D Creation from Real-World References

arXiv: 2610.23986 OpenAIAgentEmbodied AI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Recent advances in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have enabled them to act as autonomous agents, creating and editing 3D models via code or direct software manipulation. 多模态大型语言模型（ MLLM ）的最新进展使它们能够充当自主代理，通过代码或直接软件操作创建和编辑3D模型。

In practical workflows, these models must infer object shapes, materials, spatial layouts, and lighting from diverse references while strictly following instructions to reconstruct, modify, or design 3D content. 在实际工作流程中，这些模型必须从不同的参考中推断出对象形状、材质、空间布局和光照，同时严格按照说明重建、修改或设计3D内容。

However, existing benchmarks provide limited coverage of such open-ended requests across real-world scenarios. 但是，现有基准在现实场景中对此类开放式请求的覆盖范围有限。

To bridge this gap, we introduce BlenderWorld, the first comprehensive benchmark dedicated to evaluating frontier models on realistic 3D creation tasks. 为了弥合这一差距，我们引入了BlenderWorld ，这是第一个致力于评估逼真3D创建任务的前沿模型的综合基准。

BlenderWorld comprises 223 tasks spanning architecture, products, natural environments, and scientific visualization, driven by text, image, video, and structured specification inputs. BlenderWorld由223项任务组成，涵盖架构、产品、自然环境和科学可视化，由文本、图像、视频和结构化规范输入驱动。

To rigorously assess these complex deliverables, we establish a unified evaluation protocol: we render the submitted Blender projects from multiple viewpoints and evaluate them against 2,651 task-specific binary rubrics alongside holistic visual quality scores. 为了严格评估这些复杂的可交付成果，我们建立了一个统一的评估协议：我们从多个角度渲染提交的Blender项目，并根据2,651个特定任务的二元评分细则表和全面的视觉质量评分进行评估。

Benchmarking thirteen frontier models reveals that GPT-6-Astra achieves the highest overall fused score of 79.9, followed by Opus-5 (73.0) and GPT-5.6-Sol (67.7). 对13个前沿模型进行基准测试显示， GPT-6-Astra的总体融合得分最高，为79.9 ，其次是Opus-5 （ 73.0 ）和GPT-5.6-Sol （ 67.7 ）。

Ultimately, our findings reveal a critical bottleneck: while current models can parse broad instructions, they still profoundly struggle with fine-grained 3D spatial coherence and accurate structural reproduction in Blender, leaving substantial headroom for future 3D-native agentics. 最终，我们的研究结果揭示了一个关键的瓶颈：虽然当前的模型可以解析广泛的指令，但它们仍然在Blender中与精细的3D空间连贯性和准确的结构复制进行着深刻的斗争，为未来的3D原生代理留下了巨大的空间。

A3 CoEvo-RSI: When Harnesses Evolve, Which Models Should Follow?

arXiv: 2610.25078 DeepSeekQwenRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Model--harness co-evolution has emerged as a prominent paradigm for recursive self-improving (RSI) agents, jointly evolving their execution scaffolds and intrinsic capabilities. 模型- Harness协同进化已成为递归自我改善（ RSI ）代理的突出范例，共同发展其执行支架和内在能力。

We study the persistent yet often overlooked brain--body mismatch problem in this paradigm, where an evolving harness (body) demands behaviors its model (brain) has not learned or restricts capabilities it already possesses. 我们研究了这种范式中持续存在但经常被忽视的大脑-身体错配问题，在这种范式中，不断发展的线束（身体）要求其模型（大脑）尚未学习的行为或限制其已经拥有的能力。

We introduce CoEvo-RSI, a RSI framework that advances model--harness co-evolution through agent-driven, bidirectional historical inheritance. 我们引入了CoEvo-RSI ，这是一个RSI框架，它通过代理驱动的双向历史继承来推进模型- Harness协同进化。

Drawing on a shared evolutionary archive and execution feedback, a meta-agent coordinates harness development from historical designs with model checkpoint selection or merging and subsequent adaptation under the selected harness, aligning inherited capabilities with evolving execution requirements to reduce brain--body mismatch. 借助共享的进化存档和执行反馈，元代理通过模型检查点选择或合并以及所选线束下的后续适应来协调历史设计中的线束开发，使继承的能力与不断变化的执行要求保持一致，以减少脑-身体不匹配。

Using only supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on its own rejection-sampled trajectories, CoEvo-RSI makes Qwen3.5-9B competitive with larger models across diverse vertical applications, including multi-agent failure attribution, role-aware text-to-SQL, and smart-home instruction following. CoEvo-RSI仅在其自身的剔除采样轨迹上使用监督微调（ SFT ） ，使Qwen3.5-9B在不同的垂直应用中与更大的模型竞争，包括多代理失败归因、角色感知文本到SQL和智能家居指令跟踪。

It surpasses DeepSeek-V4-Pro on TraceElephant (+14.16 agent accuracy) and GLM-5.3 on HomeBench VM (+4.90 success rate), underscoring the need for brain--body alignment and the potential of bidirectional historical inheritance to advance agent co-evolution. 它超过了TraceElephant上的DeepSeek-V4-Pro （ +14.16药剂准确度）和HomeBench VM上的GLM-5.3 （ +4.90成功率） ，强调了大脑-身体对齐的必要性以及双向历史遗传的潜力，以促进药剂的共同进化。

A4 Zephyrus-R1: Agentic Reinforcement Learning for Weather Reporting

arXiv: 2610.25625 OpenAIQwenKimi

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Reinforcement learning (RL) has emerged as a key method for training AI models in scientific applications, but RL remains difficult when outputs cannot easily be verified against a known ground-truth answer. 强化学习（ RL ）已成为在科学应用中训练人工智能模型的关键方法，但当无法根据已知的地面真实答案轻松验证输出时， RL仍然很困难。

Natural-language weather forecast reports are one such domain: they translate atmospheric data into assessments of weather evolution, hazards, and uncertainty for both the general public and key decision-makers. 自然语言天气预报报告就是这样一个领域：它们将大气数据转化为公众和关键决策者对天气演变、灾害和不确定性的评估。

Unlike numerical weather-analysis questions, they admit many valid outputs, which limits deterministic verification. 与数值天气分析问题不同，它们承认许多有效的输出，这限制了确定性验证。

To address this, we develop Zephyrus-R1, a practical framework for RL on scientific weather forecast reporting. 为了解决这个问题，我们开发了Zephyrus-R1 ，这是科学天气预报报告的实用框架。

We focus on three key components of the agentic RL pipeline: reward design, curriculum learning, and an analysis of human-expert alignment. 我们专注于代理RL管道的三个关键组成部分：奖励设计、课程学习和人与专家对齐分析。

To encourage both report legibility and factual accuracy, we design a reward function that combines a meteorological rubric with bidirectional claim entailment, both evaluated against National Weather Service reference reports. 为了鼓励报告清晰度和事实准确性，我们设计了一个奖励函数，将气象分类和双向索赔相结合，两者都根据国家气象局参考报告进行了评估。

Our framework optimizes this reward in a simulated tool-calling environment, enabling vision-language agents to gather and analyze forecast evidence across regions and lead times before producing a report. 我们的框架在模拟工具调用环境中优化此奖励，使视觉语言代理能够在生成报告之前收集和分析跨地区和交付周期的预测证据。

We use annotations from three meteorologists to audit the claim labels produced by our LLM judges. 我们使用三位气象学家的注释来审核法学硕士评委制作的索赔标签。

Finally, a curriculum helps small models first develop tool use and scientific reasoning through RL on verifiable weather-analysis questions, then optimize the open-ended report reward. 最后，课程帮助小模型首先通过RL在可验证的天气分析问题上开发工具使用和科学推理，然后优化开放式报告奖励。

After training Qwen3.5-4B with Zephyrus-R1, we improve the mean across six judge-metric scores by 26% over the base model and achieve results competitive with frontier open-weight models. 在使用Zephyrus-R1训练Qwen3.5-4B之后，我们将六个判断指标的平均分比基本模型提高了26% ，并实现了与前沿开放权重模型竞争的结果。

For Qwen3.8-27B, we improve the same mean by 13% over the base model, by 6.7% over Kimi-K3, and by 2.7% over GPT-5.6-Luna. 对于Qwen3.8-27B ，我们将相同的平均值比基本模型提高了13% ，比Kimi-K3提高了6.7% ，比GPT-5.6-Luna提高了2.7%。

A5 HarnessPro: Recursive Harness Self-Improvement with Pareto Optimization for Performance-Cost Trade-offs

arXiv: 2610.26770 OpenAIAnthropicRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Harness development plays a crucial role in building capable AI agents. 线束开发在构建有能力的人工智能代理中起着至关重要的作用。

Recent approaches improve harnesses through recursive self-improvement (RSI), but their performance gains often come with higher execution costs. 最近的方法通过递归自我改进（ RSI ）来改进线束，但其性能提升通常伴随着更高的执行成本。

We introduce HarnessPro, a label-free method for evolving harnesses to improve task performance while reducing execution cost. 我们引入了HarnessPro ，这是一种无标签的方法，用于改进线束，以提高任务性能，同时降低执行成本。

In this setting, candidate harnesses are evaluated using LLM-generated tests without access to ground-truth solutions or official tests. 在此设置中，候选线束使用LLM生成的测试进行评估，而无需访问地面真相解决方案或官方测试。

Specialized agents diagnose execution trajectories, plan updates with different performance-cost priorities, and implement them as candidate harnesses. 专业代理诊断执行轨迹，使用不同的绩效成本优先级计划更新，并将其作为候选工具来实施。

Rather than returning a single harness, HarnessPro uses normalized weighted squared-distance selection to provide cost-focused (lite), balanced (medium), and performance-focused (heavy) harnesses for different deployment budgets.

Selected harnesses enter the next iteration, where execution feedback guides further updates.

Our experiments cover Terminal-Bench 2, SWE-Bench Pro, and ProgramBench, using GPT-5.5 with Codex and GLM-5.2 with Claude Code.

Across these benchmarks using GPT-5.5 with Codex, the heavy mode of HarnessPro achieves an average pass-rate gain of 2.0 percentage points and an average per-task cost reduction of 26.1% relative to RHO.

This advantage also holds when using GLM-5.2 with Claude Code.

Code is available at https://github.com/pzs19/HarnessPro

A6 Compile Once, Run Cheap: Frozen, Portable Skills for Embodied Agents, and What Recursive Self-Improvement Adds

arXiv: 2610.25114 AnthropicDeepSeekRSI

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

Embodied AI lacks the data-rich pretraining that gave language models their agents and skills, so new manipulation capability costs demonstrations per task or a frontier model in every episode.

We study a third route: a strong general model acts only as an offline author that writes, runs, diagnoses and repairs a manipulation skill in simulation until it passes an exam on layouts drawn after freezing; the frozen skill then runs as code alone or under a cheap caller that reads only its description.

We call this skill-level recursive self-improvement Skill-RSI and specify a compile protocol (isolated, audited author; hidden validation layouts; no-feedback and inherit-only controls; tamper-evident ledger) and a reuse protocol that runs the identical files with executors from another model family.

On 7 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks with cluttered tables, Claude Code compiled 4 tasks into 7 frozen skills, 5 from a first draft needing only qualification, at USD 4.29-8.22 of author cost each (USD 55 per skill counting the 13 failed campaigns).

The other three tasks did not compile in 18 campaigns, including five with GPT-6 and a pre-registered 150-layout run: on the hardest task every campaign scored 78-95 of 100 but had 3-16 safety aborts, failing the gate of 95% success with zero aborts; a 100-episode exam bounds an abort rate only to 3.6%.

On hidden layouts, repair that inherits the previous version beat from-scratch rewriting in 8 of 8 controlled campaigns (sign test p = 0.004); an inherit-only lineage attributes most of the gap to inheritance, with development feedback adding a little (3 of 3, p = 0.125).

Write-once campaigns found no detectable first-draft effect of practice (minimum detectable effect 14 points).

The frozen skills kept 148/150 successes on new layouts with no model and 147/150 with a DeepSeek-flash caller at USD 0.00028-0.00054 per episode; no direct-control baseline was run.

A7 MOSAIC: More LLMs, Less Cost through Collaborative Reasoning for Compiler Optimizations

arXiv: 2602.01935 OpenAIMetaAgent

📝 中文摘要（中英对照）：

LLM-guided compiler optimization has recently shown promise.

However, current LLM-guided optimizers query a single LLM at every search step, so their cost grows with both that model's price and the search budget.

A smaller model is cheaper but degrades the search: in our experiments, a recent single-LLM optimizer reaches 10.2% (6.0%) lower speedup on GPU (CPU) with gpt-5-mini than with GPT-5.2.

Agentic multi-LLM frameworks add controllers, concurrent calls, and shared memory, whose overhead can offset the savings of cheaper models.

This paper shows that the optimization search tree itself can coordinate heterogeneous LLMs: the tree already records which proposals improved the program, and we can augment the tree to record which model made them.

That evidence can then be used to decide which model should propose next.

To this end, we introduce MOSAIC, in which each Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) node records a program and the LLM that expands it, and the active LLM proposes both a compiler transformation and the LLM for the next step.

Existing methods do not jointly provide: (i) principled inference of each agent's implicit values from behavior, (ii) compositional formal safety guarantees without full online communication, and (iii) value-aware conflict resolution with faithful explanations.

We present VACS (Value-Aligned Compositional Shielding), a four-layer framework addressing all three.

Layer 1 learns value-dimension rewards from pairwise preferences using Bradley-Terry modeling and infers per-agent value weights via deep MaxEnt IRL.

Layer 2 encodes value constraints in a Lean-inspired DSL and synthesizes compositional assume-guarantee shields for runtime safety.

Layer 3 resolves disagreement through nucleolus-based credit allocation and Hamiltonian consensus optimization under long-term value constraints.

Layer 4 extracts a critical reasoning path from co-state sensitivities and generates formally grounded natural-language explanations.

Our contribution is primarily a unified systems design with formalized interfaces and operational guarantees at the verifier-constrained decision level, rather than a complete end-to-end formal proof of all language-model internals.

In controlled proof-of-concept evaluations with role-conditioned agent panels on NEJM-AI QA, MathInstruct-Subset, and a cybersecurity incident-response benchmark (CyberSec-Eval), VACS outperforms strong baselines in accuracy (85.4%, 95.0%, and 90.0%) while reducing logical inconsistency rates to near zero.
